    Pull-Up/Pull-Down Maneuver

    #Equations of Motion

    For pull-up and pull-down maneuvers, we are interested only in the
    instantaneous turn radius and turn rate at the start of the maneuver.

    #Pull-Up Maneuver

    Using Newton’s second law in the y-direction,

    mar​=L−W

    mRV2​=L−W

    mgRmV2​=WL​−1

    gRV2​=n−1

    💡
    R=g(n−1)V2​
    💡
    ω=Vg(n−1)​

    For a given flight speed, an aircraft that can support a higher load factor
    is capable of performing a faster pull-up maneuver with a smaller radius.

    #Pull-Down Maneuver

    Using Newton’s second law in the y-direction,

    mar​=L+W

    mRV2​=L+W

    mgRmV2​=WL​+1

    gRV2​=n+1

    💡
    R=g(n+1)V2​
    💡
    ω=Vg(n+1)​

    For a given flight speed, an aircraft that can support a higher load factor is
    capable of performing a faster pull-down maneuver with a smaller radius.

    #Comparison with Level Turn

    Level turn equations:

    R=gn2−1​V2​

    ω=Vgn2−1​​

    Pull-up equations:

    R=g(n−1)V2​

    ω=Vg(n−1)​

    Pull-down equations:

    R=g(n+1)V2​

    ω=Vg(n+1)​

    If the load factor n is extremely large, the equations for each
    flight condition would give the same turn radius and turn rate.

    💡
    R=gnV2​
    💡
    ω=Vgn​

    #Corner/Maneuver Speed

    Recall from level turn that the maneuver point corresponds to the smallest possible turn
    radius and highest possible turn rate. It is the intersection point of the stall boundary
    with the structural boundary.

    At the maneuver point, lift coefficient and load factor are at their highest possible values.

    L=21​ρV2SCLmax​​

    nmax​W=21​ρV2SCLmax​​

    Corner speed can be computed by subsituting the maximum values of n and CL​.

    💡
    Vcorner​=ρSCLmax​​2nmax​W​​

    The red-line speed, or dive speed, forms the right boundary of the V-n diagram. This speed
    is 1.2 times the maximum level flight speed     Vmax​. Beyond this speed, aeroelastic instabilities
    such as wing flutter, aileron reversal, buffeting, and panel flutter can occur. These instabilities
    can cause unwanted vibrations that can result in failure of components.
