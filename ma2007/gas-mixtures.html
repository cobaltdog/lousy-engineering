    Gas Mixtures

    #Mass and Mole Fractions

    Composition of a mixture:

    • Molar analysis — specifiying the number of moles of each component
    • Gravimetric analysis — specifiying the mass of each component

    Total mass of a mixture is,

    💡
    mmix​=i=1∑k​mi​

    Mass fraction is,

    💡
    mfi​=mmix​mi​​=yi​(Mmix​Mi​​)

    Mole number of a mixture is,

    💡
    Nmix​=i=1∑k​Ni​

    Mole fraction is,

    💡
    yi​=Nmix​Ni​​

    Mass of a substance is the product of mole number N and molar mass M, as m=NM.

    💡
    Mmix​=Nmix​mmix​​=Nmix​∑mi​​=Nmix​∑Ni​Mi​​=i=1∑k​yi​Mi​

    The above equation is the apparant molar mass of a mixture expressed in kg/kmol.

    The gas constant of a mixture can be expressed as,

    💡
    Rmix​=Mmix​Ru​​

    #P-v-T of Gas Mixtures

    Dalton’s law of additive pressures:

    Pressure of a gas mixture is equal to the sum of the pressures each gas would exert if it existed alone at the mixture temperature and volume.

    💡
    Pmix​=i=1∑k​Pi​(Tmix​,Vˉmix​)

    Amagat’s law of additive volumes:

    Volume of a gas mixture is equal to the sum of the volumes each gas would occupy if it existed alone at the mixture temperature and pressure.

    💡
    Vˉmix​=i=1∑k​Vˉi​(Tmix​,Pmix​)

    Note that Vˉi​ is the volume a component would occupy if it existed alone at Tmix​ and Pmix​,
    and not the actual volume occupied by the component in the mixture. In a vessel that holds
    a gas mixture, each component fills the entire volume of the vessel. Thus, volume of each component is equal to the volume of the vessel.

    #Ideal Gas Mixtures

    Using the ideal-gas relation,

    Pmix​Pi​​=Nmix​Ru​Tmix​/Vˉmix​Ni​Ru​Tmix​/Vˉmix​​=Nmix​Ni​​

    Vˉmix​Vˉi​​=Nmix​Ru​Tmix​/Pmix​Ni​Ru​Tmix​/Pmix​​=Nmix​Ni​​

    💡
    Pmix​Pi​​=Vˉmix​Vˉi​​=Nmix​Ni​​=yi​

    #Properties of Gas Mixtures

    Total internal energy, enthalpy, and entropy of a gas mixture can be expressed as,

    💡
    Umix​=i=1∑k​Ui​=i=1∑k​mi​ui​
    💡
    Hmix​=i=1∑k​Hi​=i=1∑k​mi​hi​
    💡
    Smix​=i=1∑k​Si​=i=1∑k​mi​si​

    Internal energy. enthalpy, and entropy of a mixture per unit mass or per unit mole is,

    💡
    umix​=i=1∑k​mfi​ui​
    💡
    hmix​=i=1∑k​mfi​hi​
    💡
    smix​=i=1∑k​mfi​si​
    💡
    uˉmix​=i=1∑k​yi​uˉi​
    💡
    hˉmix​=i=1∑k​yi​hˉi​
    💡
    sˉmix​=i=1∑k​yi​sˉi​

    Similarly, the specfic heats of a gas mixture can be expressed as,

    💡
    cv(mix)​=i=1∑k​mfi​cv,i​
    💡
    cˉv(mix)​=i=1∑k​yi​cˉv,i​
    💡
    cp(mix)​=i=1∑k​mfi​cp,i​
    💡
    cˉp(mix)​=i=1∑k​yi​cˉp,i​
