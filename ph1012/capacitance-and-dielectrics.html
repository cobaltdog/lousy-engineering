    Capacitance and Dielectrics

    A capacitor is a device that stores electric potential energy and electric charge.

    #Capacitors and Capacitance

    Any two conductors separated by an insulator form a capacitor.

    Capacitance is a measure of the ability of a capacitor to store energy.

    💡
    C=Vdiff​Q​

    The SI unit of capacitance is farad F. One farad is equal to one coulomb per volt C/V.

    #Parallel Plate Capacitor

    The simplest form of a capacitor consists of two parallel conducting plates separated by a distance d that is small in comparison with their dimensions.

    When the plates are charged, the electric field between the plates is essentially uniform, and the charges on the plates are uniformly distributed over their opposing surfaces.

    #Dielectrics

    Functions of a dielectric:

    • Solves the mechanical problem of maintaining two large metal sheets without contact
    • Increases the maximum possible potential difference between the capacitor plates
    • Capacitance is greater when there is a dielectric material than when there is vacuum

    The original capacitance without a dielectric is C0​.

    The capacitance with the dielectric present is C.

    C0​=V0​Q​

    C=VQ​

    💡
    K=C0​C​

    The ratio of C to C0​ is the dielectric constant.

    K=VQ​×QV0​​

    💡
    V=KV0​​

    When the charge is constant, the potential difference for a given charge is reduced by a factor of K with the dielectric present.

    The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor filled with a dielectric between the plates can be expressed as
