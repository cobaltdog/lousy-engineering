    Energy Conservation

    Energy can be converted from one form to another but cannot be created or destroyed.

    #Gravitational Potential Energy

    Work done by gravity is positive when the body moves downwards as w and s are in the same direction.

    💡
    Ugrav​=mgy

    Work done by gravity is negative when the body moves upwards as w and s are in opposite directions.

    Gain in GPE=mg(y2​−y1​)

    mg(y2​−y1​)=Ugrav,f​−Ugrav,i​

    −Wgrav​=mgΔy

    💡
    Wgrav​=−ΔUgrav​

    #Conservation of Mechanical Energy

    If a body’s weight is the only force acting on it, the total work done on the body
    equals the change in the body’s kinetic energy, and also equals the change in
    the body’s gravitational potential energy.

    Wtot​=Wgrav​

    Wtot​=ΔK=Kf​−Ki​

    Wgrav​=−ΔUgrav​=Ugrav,i​−Ugrav,f​

    💡
    Ki​+Ugrav,i​=Kf​+Ugrav,f​
    💡
    21​mv1​2+mgy1​=21​mv2​2+mgy2​

    The sum of kinetic and potential energy is called E — total mechanical energy of the system. When only the force of gravity does work, the total mechanical energy is constant.

    #Elastic Potential Energy

    💡
    Uel​=21​kx2

    Work done by the elastic force can be expressed in terms of the change in elastic potential energy.

    Wel​=21​kx1​2−21​kx2​2

    −Wel​=21​kx2​2−21​kx1​2

    💡
    Wel​=−ΔUel​

    #Conservation of Mechanical Energy

    If the elastic force is the only force that does work on a body, the total work done equals the change in the body’s elastic potential energy.

    Kf​−Ki​=Uel,i​−Uel,f​

    💡
    Ki​+Uel,i​=Kf​+Uel,f​
    💡
    21​mv1​2+21​kx1​2=21​mv2​2+21​kx2​2

    If both gravitational and elastic forces does work, as well as other forces such as air resistance, the total work is the sum of the work done by each type of force.

    Ki​+Uel,1​+Ugrav,1​+Wother​=K2​+Uel,2​+Ugrav,2​

    💡
    Ki​+Ui​+Wother​=Kf​+Uf​

    #Law of Conservation

    #Conservative Forces

    A force that offers the opportunity of two-way conversion between kinetic and potential energies is called a conservative force.

    Properties of conservative forces:

    • Can be expressed as a difference between the initial and final energies
    • Independent of path taken and depends on starting and ending points
    • Total work is zero if starting and ending points are the same
    • Conversion of energies is reversible

    When only conservative forces do work, the total mechanical energy is constant.

    💡
    E=K+U

    #Non-Conservative Forces

    Energy lost to non-conservative forces cannot be recovered by reversing the motion or in any other way, and the total mechanical energy is not conserved. Thus, the work done by non-conservative forces cannot be represented by a potential-energy function.

    When a car with locked brakes skids to a stop, the tires and the road surface both become hotter. The energy associated with this change in state of materials is called internal energy.

    💡
    ΔUint​=−Wother​
    💡
    ΔK+ΔU+ΔUint​=0

    #Force and Potential Energy

    Let’s consider motion along a straight line, with coordinate x. We denote the x-component of force by Fx​(x), and the potential energy as U(x). Recall that in any displacement, the work done by a conservative force equals the negative of the change in potential energy.

    W=−ΔU

    Fx​(x) Δx=−ΔU

    Fx​(x)=−ΔxΔU​

    💡
    Fx​=−dxdU​
