    6-DOF Modelling of Aircraft

    #Coordinate Systems

    #Inertial Axis System (Oxi​yi​zi​)

    Inertial axis system:

    • Coordinate system at rest or moving with uniform velocity
    • A system with origin fixed to the center of the Earth
    • Axes do not rotate with the Earth
    • Earth-centered axis system

    #Earth-Fixed Axis System (OxE​yE​zE​)

    Earth-fixed axis system:

    • Origin located at the center of the Earth and axes rotate with the Earth
    • xE​-axis passes through intersection of meridian and equator
    • zE​-axis aligned along Earth’s axis of rotation
    • yE​-axis is perpendicular to zE​ and xE​ axes
    • Often used as inertial axis system

    #Navigational Axis System (Oxe​ye​ze​)

    Navigational axis system:

    • Origin located on the surface of the Earth and axes rotate with the Earth
    • Origin is chosen as the point directly below the aircraft at time t=0
    • ze​-axis points towards the center of the Earth
    • xe​-axis points towards the local North
    • ye​-axis points towards the local East
    • Often used as inertia axis system

    #Body-Fixed Axis System (Oxb​yb​zb​)

    Body-fixed axis system:

    • Origin located at the aircraft CG and axes are fixed to the aircraft body
    • xb​-axis aligned along the fuselage reference line or zero-lift line
    • zb​-axis aligned along the yaw axis
    • yb​-axis aligned along the pitch axis

    #Wind Axis System (Oxw​yw​zw​)

    Wind axis system:

    • Origin located at the aircraft CG and axes are fixed to the aircraft body
    • xw​-axis points in the opposite direction of the relative wind vector
    • zw​-axis lies on vertical plane of symmetry, perpendicular to xw​
    • yw​-axis points right and is perpendicular to xw​ and zw​

    To convert from wind axis to body-fixed axis system:

    • Rotate wind axis about zw​ by sideslip angle β
    • Rotate wind axis about yw​ by angle of attack α

    #Stability Axis System (Oxs​ys​zs​)

    Stability axis system:

    • Special case of wind axis system
    • xs​-axis points opposite to the relative wind vector
    • For β=0 the xs​-axis lies on the vertical plane of symmetry
    • For β=0 the xs​-axis is projected onto the vertical plane of symmetry
    • zs​-axis lies on vertical plane of symmetry, perpendicular to xs​
    • ys​-axis points right and is perpendicular to xs​ and zs​

    #Aircraft Position and Orientation

    Initial state of aircraft:

    • Body-fixed system Oxb​yb​zb​ coincides with chosen inertial axis system Oxi​yi​zi​
    • Aircraft position during flight is described by coordinates (xiCG​​, yiCG​​, ziCG​​)
    • Aircraft orientation during flight is described by Euler angles (ψ, θ, ϕ)
    • Body-fixed axes are rotated successively to obtain final orientation

    The final orientation of the aircraft is obtained by rotating in the 3-2-1 sequence.

    The number 3 represents rotation about the zi​-axis.
    The number 2 represents rotation about the     yi​-axis.
    The number 1 represents rotation about the     xi​-axis.

    Yaw rotation:

    • Rotate the aircraft about zi​ by an angle ψ
    • Rotated body-fixed axis denoted as (x1​, y1​, z1​)
    • Range of rotation angles is −π≤ψ≤π

    Pitch rotation:

    • Rotate the aircraft about y1​ by angle θ
    • Rotated body-fixed axis denoted as (x2​, y2​, z2​)
    • Range of rotation angles is −2π​≤θ≤2π​

    Roll rotation:

    • Rotate the aircraft about x2​ by angle ϕ
    • Rotated body-fixed axis denoted as (xb​, yb​, zb​)
    • Range of rotation angles is −π≤ϕ≤π

    #Rotation Matrices

    Yaw rotation matrix,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​x1​y1​z1​​⎭⎬⎫​=​cosψ−sinψ0​sinψcosψ0​001​​⎩⎨⎧​xi​yi​zi​​⎭⎬⎫​=Tψ​⎩⎨⎧​xi​yi​zi​​⎭⎬⎫​

    where Tψ​≡T1i​ is the rotation matrix,
    conversion from inertia axis     i to single-axis 1

    Pitch rotation matrix,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​x2​y2​z2​​⎭⎬⎫​=​cosθ0sinθ​010​−sinθ0cosθ​​⎩⎨⎧​x1​y1​z1​​⎭⎬⎫​=Tθ​⎩⎨⎧​x1​y1​z1​​⎭⎬⎫​

    where Tθ​≡T21​ is the rotation matrix,
    conversion from single-axis     1 to two-axis 2

    Roll rotation matrix,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​xb​yb​zb​​⎭⎬⎫​=​100​0cosϕ−sinϕ​0sinϕcosϕ​​⎩⎨⎧​x2​y2​z2​​⎭⎬⎫​=Tϕ​⎩⎨⎧​x2​y2​z2​​⎭⎬⎫​

    where Tϕ​≡Tb2​ is the rotation matrix,
    conversion from two-axis     2 to body-fixed axis b

    #Transformation Matrices

    Coordinate transform, inertia axis → body-fixed axis,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​xb​yb​zb​​⎭⎬⎫​=Tϕ​Tθ​Tψ​⎩⎨⎧​xi​yi​zi​​⎭⎬⎫​=Tb2​T21​T1i​⎩⎨⎧​xi​yi​zi​​⎭⎬⎫​

    Coordinate transform, inertia axis → body-fixed axis,

    💡

    Use the above equation if the Euler angles ψ, θ, ϕ are non-zero.

    💡

    #Linear Velocity Transformation

    Transformation of linear velocity is similar to coordinate transformation.

    Velocity transform, inertia axis → body-fixed axis,

    💡

    Velocity transform, body-fixed axis → inertia axis

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​x˙i​y˙​i​z˙i​​⎭⎬⎫​=​Cθ​Cψ​Cθ​Sψ​−Sθ​​Sϕ​Sθ​Cψ​−Cϕ​Sψ​Sϕ​Sθ​Sψ​+Cϕ​Cψ​Sϕ​Cθ​​Cϕ​Sθ​Cψ​+Sϕ​Sψ​Cϕ​Sθ​Sψ​−Sϕ​Cψ​Cϕ​Cθ​​​⎩⎨⎧​uvw​⎭⎬⎫​

    #Angular Velocity Transformation

    Components of aircraft’s angular velocity in body-fixed axis,

    ω=⎩⎨⎧​ωxb​​ωyb​​ωzb​​​⎭⎬⎫​=⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​

    p denotes roll rate (rotation about xb​)
    q denotes pitch rate (rotation about yb​)
    r denotes yaw rate (rotation about zb​)

    Euler rates (ψ˙​, θ˙, ϕ˙​) are rotational rates about zi​, y1​, and x2​ axes respectively.

    • ψ˙​ is measured in the inertial frame (inertia axis i → 1 → 2 → body-fixed axis b)
    • θ˙ is measured in frame 1 (1 → 2 → body-fixed axis b)
    • ϕ˙​ is measured in frame 2 (2 → body-fixed axis b)

    Angular velocity transform, inertia axis → body-fixed axis,

    ⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​=Tϕ​⎩⎨⎧​ϕ˙​00​⎭⎬⎫​+Tϕ​Tθ​⎩⎨⎧​0θ˙0​⎭⎬⎫​+Tϕ​Tθ​Tψ​⎩⎨⎧​00ψ˙​​⎭⎬⎫​

    Angular velocity transform, inertia axis → body-fixed axis,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​=​100​0cosϕ−sinϕ​−sinθsinϕcosθcosϕcosθ​​⎩⎨⎧​ϕ˙​θ˙ψ˙​​⎭⎬⎫​

    Angular velocity transform, body-fixed axis → inertia axis,

    💡

    ⎩⎨⎧​ϕ˙​θ˙ψ˙​​⎭⎬⎫​=​100​sinϕtanθcosϕsinϕsecθ​cosϕtanθ−sinϕcosϕsecθ​​⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​

    #Aircraft Equations of Motion (EoM)

    Choice of axis system:

    • Newton’s 2nd law is valid only in an inertia axis system
    • Aircraft sensors take measurements w.r.t. body-fixed axis system
    • A need to define velocity components in the body-fixed axis system,
      but express the time derivative of momentum in the inertia axis system

    General equation for total time derivative,

    💡

    (dtdA​)inertia​=(dtdA​)rotating​+ω×A

    where ω is the angular velocity vector of the rotating frame

    Aircraft velocity in the inertia axis system,

    V=x˙i^i​+y˙​j^​i​+z˙k^i​

    Aircraft velocity in the body-fixed axis system,

    V=ui^b​+vj^​b​+wk^b​

    Total time derivative of aircraft velocity,

    (dtdV​)i​=dtdu​i^b​+dtdv​j^​b​+dtdw​k^b​+udtdi^b​​+vdtdj^​b​​+wdtdk^b​​

    (dtdV​)i​=dtdu​i^b​+dtdv​j^​b​+dtdw​k^b​+u(ω×i^b​)+v(ω×j^​b​)+w(ω×k^b​)

    (dtdV​)i​=dtdu​i^b​+dtdv​j^​b​+dtdw​k^b​+ω×(ui^b​+vj^​b​+wk^b​)

    (dtdV​)i​=dtdu​i^b​+dtdv​j^​b​+dtdw​k^b​+ω×V

    💡

    (dtdV​)i​=(dtdV​)b​+ω×V

    #Derivation of EoM

    Assumptions:

    • Aircraft is a rigid body with a constant mass m
    • Aircraft has a vertical plane of symmetry passing through longitudinal axis and CG

    Body-Fixed Axis System
    Linear velocityV=ui^b​+vj^​b​+wk^b​
    Angular velocityω=pi^b​+qj^​b​+rk^b​
    External forcesFext​=Xi^b​+Yj^​b​+Zk^b​
    External momentsMext​=Li^b​+Mj^​b​+Nk^b​

    Roll rate p, pitch rate q, and yaw rate r.
    Roll moment     L, pitch moment M, and yaw moment N.

    Linear momentum of the aircraft,

    💡

    L=mV=m(ui^b​+vj^​b​+wk^b​)

    Angular momentum of the aircraft,

    H=Iω

    H=​Ixx​−Ixy​−Ixz​​−Ixy​Iyy​−Iyz​​−Ixz​−Iyz​Izz​​​⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​

    H=⎩⎨⎧​pIxx​−qIxy​−rIxz​−pIxy​+qIyy​−rIyz​−pIxz​−qIyz​+rIzz​​⎭⎬⎫​

    Aircraft is symmetrical about the vertical plane such that Ixy​=0 and Iyz​=0.

    💡

    H=(pIxx​−rIxz​)i^b​+(qIyy​)j^​b​+(−pIxz​+rIzz​)k^b​

    External forces on the aircraft,

    Fext​=ma=dtd​mV=dtd​L

    Fext​=(dtdL​)b​+ω×L

    Fext​=⎩⎨⎧​mu˙mv˙mw˙​⎭⎬⎫​+⎩⎨⎧​pqr​⎭⎬⎫​×⎩⎨⎧​mumvmw​⎭⎬⎫​

    Fext​=⎩⎨⎧​mu˙mv˙mw˙​⎭⎬⎫​+​i^pmu​j^​qmv​k^rmw​​

    💡

    X=m(u˙+qw−rv)

    Y=m(v˙+ru−pw)

    Z=m(w˙+pv−qu)

    External moments on the aircraft,

    Mext​=Iα=dtd​Iω=dtd​H

    Mext​=(dtdH​)b​+ω×H

    💡

    L=Ixx​p˙​−Ixz​r˙+(Izz​−Iyy​)qr−Ixz​pq

    M=Iyy​q˙​+(Ixx​−Izz​)pr+Ixz​(p2−r2)

    N=−Ixz​p˙​+Izz​r˙+(Iyy​−Ixx​)pq+Ixz​qr
