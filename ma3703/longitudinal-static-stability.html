    MA3703 · FLIGHT DYNAMICS

    Longitudinal Static Stability

    #Pitching Moment

    Steady pitch moment of an aircraft about its CG is affected by:

    • Angle of attack α
    • Elevator deflection δe​

    Equation for pitching moment,

    💡
    Cm​(α, δe​)=Cm0​​+Cmα​​α+Cmδe​​​δe​

    where Cmα​​=dαdCm​​ and Cmδe​​​=dδe​Cm​​

    A change in pitching moment would result from changes in α and δe​.

    💡
    ΔCm​(α, δe​)=Cmα​​Δα+Cmδe​​​Δδe​

    When an aircraft is in equilibrium in longitudinal flight, at an angle of attack αtrim​ and
    an elevator deflection δetrim​​, the total pitching moment of the aircraft should be zero.

    💡
    Cm​(αtrim​, δetrim​​)=0

    #Static Stability Criterion

    Longitudinal static stability:

    • Only concerned with the initial pitching moment response
      tendency of an aircraft after an angle of attack disturbance
    • Statically stable aircraft generates an opposite moment to AoA disturbance
    • Statically neutral aircraft generates zero moment in response to AoA disturbance
    • Statically unstable aircraft generates a moment in the same direction as disturbance

    In the stick-fixed condition, the elevator is held fixed at its trim setting.
    In stick-fixed case, it is often assumed that elevator deflection δe​=0.

    Equation for pitching moment becomes,

    💡
    Cm​(α)=Cm0​​+Cmα​​α

    where Cm0​​ is the pitching moment coefficient when α=0

    Observations (statically stable):

    • Aircraft is flying at αtrim​ and experiences an increase in angle of attack
    • Cm​ of the aircraft decreases, a negative moment to restore the AoA
    • Aircraft is flying at αtrim​ and experiences a decrease in angle of attack
    • Cm​ of the aircraft increases, a positive moment to restore the AoA

    Observations (statically unstable):

    • Aircraft is flying at αtrim​ and experiences an increase in angle of attack
    • Cm​ of the aircraft increases, a positive moment increases AoA further
    • Aircraft is flying at αtrim​ and experiences a decrease in angle of attack
    • Cm​ of the aircraft decreases, a negative moment decreases AoA further

    Criterion for longitudinal static stability,

    💡
    dαdCm​​≡Cmα​​<0

    Gradient of the pitching moment variation graph must be negative.

    #Additional Criteria

    Trimmed lift is preferred to be at a positive angle of attack.
    For a statically stable aircraft, a useful trim point requires,

    💡
    Cm0​​>0

    Aircraft can be trimmed with a positive angle of attack, shown by
    the positive     x-intercept in the pitching moment variation graph.

    Criterion for longitudinal static stability can be rewritten as,

    Cmα​​=dαdCm​​=dCL​dCm​​⋅dαdCL​​=dCL​dCm​​CLα​​

    Recall that CLα​​ is the lift slope.

    Since lift slope CLα​​>0, an alternative criterion is,

    💡
    dCL​dCm​​<0

    #Contribution of Components

    Total pitching moment of an aircraft about its CG can be summarized as,

    💡
    CmCG​​=CmCGwing​​​+CmCGtail​​​+CmCGfuselage​​​+CmCGpropulsion​​​

    For an aircraft in equilibrium in longitudinal flight,

    Cm​(α)=Cm0​​+Cmα​​α

    Cm0​​=Cm0w​​​+Cm0t​​​+Cm0f​​​+Cm0p​​​

    Cmα​​=Cmαw​​​+Cmαt​​​+Cmαf​​​+Cmαp​​​

    #Wing Contribution

    Wing contribution to pitching moment of an aircraft is due to lift, drag, and moment.

    Taking moments about the aircraft CG (pitch up as positive),

    Mw​=Mlift​+Mdrag​+Macw​​

    Mw​=Lw​cos(αw​−iw​)(xCG​−xac​)+Lw​sin(αw​−iw​)zCG​           +Dw​sin(αw​−iw​)(xCG​−xac​)−Dw​cos(αw​−iw​)zCG​+Macw​​

    Dividing the above by 21​ρV2Scˉ,

    Cmw​​=CLw​​cos(αw​−iw​)(cˉxCG​−xac​​)+CLw​​sin(αw​−iw​)cˉzCG​​            +CDw​​sin(αw​−iw​)(cˉxCG​−xac​​)−CDw​​cos(αw​−iw​)cˉzCG​​+Cmacw​​​

    Assumptions:

    • Small angles such that cos(αw​−iw​)≈1
    • Small angles such that sin(αw​−iw​)=αw​−iw​
    • Negligible vertical distance from CG such that cˉxCG​​≫cˉzCG​​
    • Lift coefficient of wing much larger than its drag coefficient CLw​​≫CDw​​

    Wing contribution to total pitching moment simplifies to,

    💡
    Cmw​​=CLw​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)+Cmacw​​​

    Recall that lift coefficient can be expressed as,

    CLw​​=CL0w​​​+CLαw​​​αw​

    Substituting the lift coefficient expression,

    Cmw​​=(CL0w​​​+CLαw​​​αw​)(cˉxCG​−xac​​)+Cmacw​​​

    Cmw​​=Cm0w​​​+Cmαw​​​αw​ (linear form of equation)

    Expressing the above in a linear form,

    💡
    Cm0w​​​=Cmacw​​​+CL0w​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)
    💡
    Cmαw​​​=CLαw​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)

    #Stability of a Wing Alone

    For a wing alone to be statically stable,

    Cmαw​​​=CLαw​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)<0

    Since wing lift coefficient CLαw​​​>0,

    xCG​<xac​

    Stabilizing: wing’s aerodynamic center is located aft of the CG.
    Destabilizing: wing’s aerodynamic center is located forward of the     CG.

    To trim a wing alone at a positive α,

    Cm0w​​​=Cmacw​​​+CL0w​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)>0

    Since CL0w​​​>0 and xCG​−xac​<0:

    • Cmaw​​​ must be strongly positive
    • Achievable through a negative-cambered airfoil
    • Negative-cambered airfoil has lower CLmax​​ and reduced range of usable α
    • Most aircraft employ use of positive-cambered airfoil for better lift characteristics
    • Most aircraft also locate their CG slightly aft of aerodynamic center for efficiency
    • In conclusion, the wing contribution is typically destabilizing, a tail is thus required

    #Tail Contribution

    Horizontal tail is affected by wing flow field:

    • Bound vortex and wing tip vortices
    • Vortices create a downwash field at and behind the wing
    • Local angle of attack of the tail is lower than freestream angle of attack

    Total lift generated,

    L=Lw​+Lt​

    L=21​ρVw​2Sw​CLw​​+21​ρVt​2St​CLt​​

    Dividing the above by 21​ρVw​2Sw​,

    CL​=CLw​​+21​ρVw​221​ρVt​2​(Sw​St​​)CLt​​

    CL​=CLw​​+Qw​Qt​​(Sw​St​​)CLt​​

    💡
    CL​=CLw​​+ηH​Sw​St​​CLt​​

    where ηH​ is the horizontal tail efficiency

    If Qt​<Qw​ tail is located in the wake region of the wing, momentum loss in the wake.
    If Qt​>Qw​ tail is located in the slipstream of a propeller, momentum gain in the wake.

    Momentum loss due to disturbed airflow over the tail (wake of the wing).
    Momentum gain due to higher energy airflow caused by propeller or jet.

    Angle of attack of the aircraft,

    α=αFRL​=αw​−iw​

    Angle of attack of the tail,

    αt​=αFRL​−ε+it​

    αt​=αw​−iw​−ε+it​

    Taking moments about the aircraft CG (pitch up as positive),

    Mt​=Mlift​+Mdrag​+Mact​​

    Mt​=−lt​[Lt​cos(α−ε)]−zCGt​​[Lt​sin(α−ε)]           +lt​[Dt​sin(α−ε)]+zCGt​​[Dt​cos(α−ε)]+Mact​​

    Assumptions:

    • Small angles
    • Lift greater than drag such that Lt​≫Dt​
    • Negligible vertical distance from CG such that lt​≫zCGt​​
    • Negligible moment about the tail’s aerodynamic center Mact​​

    Tail contribution to pitching moment simplifies to,

    💡
    Mt​=−lt​Lt​=−lt​(21​ρVt​2St​CLt​​)

    Dividing the above by 21​ρVw​2Sw​cˉ,

    Cmt​​=−cˉlt​​⋅21​ρVw​221​ρVt​2​(Sw​St​​)CLt​​

    Cmt​​=−cˉlt​​⋅ηH​(Sw​St​​)CLt​​

    Cmt​​=−ηH​(cˉlt​​⋅Sw​St​​)CLt​​

    💡
    Cmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLt​​

    where VH​ is the horizontal tail volume ratio

    Assume tail’s airfoil is symmetric such that CL0t​​​=0 when tail angle of attack αt​=0.

    CLt​​=CLαt​​​αt​=CLαt​​​(αw​−iw​−ε+it​)

    where downwash angle ε=ε0​+dαdε​αw​,
    and     ε0​ is the downwash angle at αw​=0

    Substituting the lift coefficient of the tail,

    Cmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLt​​

    Cmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(αw​−iw​−ε+it​)

    Cmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​[αw​−iw​−(ε0​+dαdε​αw​)+it​]

    Cmt​​=ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(ε0​+iw​−it​)−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(1−dαdε​)αw​

    Cmt​​=Cm0t​​​+Cmαt​​​αw​ (linear form of equation)

    Expressing the above in a linear form,

    💡
    Cm0t​​​=ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(ε0​+iw​−it​)
    💡
    Cmαt​​​=−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(1−dαdε​)

    where tail lift slope CLαt​​​=1+a0​/(πeAR)a0​​

    To achieve a certain degree of aircraft longitudinal static stability Cmα​​:

    • Proper selection of VH​ by adjusting the values of St​ and Sw​
    • Proper selection of CLαt​​​ by adjusting aspect ratio of the tail

    To achieve aircraft trimming at positive angle of attack Cm0t​​​>0:

    • Adjust tail incidence angle it​ to yield the desired Cm0t​​​
    • More negative it​ results in a more positive Cm0t​​​
    • Horizontal stabilizer typically set at negative incidence angle

    #Canard Contribution

    Canard contribution:

    • Affected by upwash instead of downwash
    • Upwash effect is usually smaller than downwash
    • Relatively free from propulsive flow interferences and wing wake
    • Produces positive lift to counter wing’s nose-down pitching moment
    • Use of canard reduces wing loading/size and thus, less induced drag from wing

    Effect on longitudinal static stability:

    • Canard is destabilizing
    • If aircraft pitches up due to a disturbance, canard α increases
    • Higher α results in more lift generated by canard and aircraft pitches up further

    Design of canard:

    • Usually has a higher incidence angle compared to the wing
    • At high α, stalls before the wing and loses lift, causing nose to drop and exit stall

    #Propulsion Contribution

    Thrust line of the engines creates a pitching moment about the aircraft CG.
    A thrust line offset zp​ creates a pitching moment that contributes to Cm0​​.
    The moment contribution is independent of the aircraft angle of attack α.

    Propeller/engine inlet normal force:

    • Propeller at an angle of attack will develop a normal force Np​ in the plane of rotation
    • Np​ creates a pitching moment that is dependent on the angle of attack
    • Pitching moment generated will contribute to Cmα​​

    Simplified pitching moment about aircraft CG due to propulsion,

    💡

    Mp​=Tzp​+Np​lp​

    #Total Aircraft Pitching Moment

    Assumptions:

    • Thrust vectors act through aircraft CG, no pitching moment contribution due to thrust
    • Other power effects due to engine, nacelle, and propeller are ignored
    • Drag contribution to pitching moment is neglected
    • Aft tail configuration

    Total pitching moment of an aircraft about its CG is simplified to,

    💡

    Cm​=Cmw​​+Cmt​​+Cmf​​

    Pitching moment equation based on wing angle of attack αw​,

    💡

    Cm​=Cm0​​+Cm(αw​)​​(αw​)

    Cm0​​=Cmacw​​​+CL0w​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)+ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(ε0​+iw​−it​)+Cm0f​​​

    Cm(αw​)​​=CLαw​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(1−dαdε​)+Cmαf​​​

    Pitching moment equation based on aircraft angle of attack α,

    💡

    Cm​=Cm0​​+Cmα​​α

    Cm0​​=Cmacw​​​+(CL0w​​​+CLαw​​​iw​)(cˉxCG​−xac​​)            +ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(ε0​+dαdε​iw​−it​)+Cm0f​​​+Cmαf​​​iw​

    Cmα​​=CLαw​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(1−dαdε​)+Cmαf​​​

    Pitching moment equation based on lift coefficient,

    Cm​=Cm0​​+dαabs​dCm​​αabs​

    Cm​=Cm0​​+dCL​dCm​​⋅dαabs​dCL​​αabs​

    💡

    Cm​=Cm0​​+CmCL​​​CL​

    #Stick-Fixed Neutral Point

    Stick-fixed is where the longitudinal control (elevator) is held fixed at its trim setting.

    The neutral point is the location of the CG at which Cmα​​=0.

    0=CLαw​​​(cˉxCG​−xac​​)−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(1−dαdε​)+Cmαf​​​

    Rearranging the above to determine the neutral point,

    💡

    cˉxNP​​=cˉxac​​−CLαw​​​Cmαf​​​​+ηH​VH​CLαw​​​CLαt​​​​(1−dαdε​)

    Neutral point is essentially the “aerodynamic center” of the entire aircraft.

    #Static Margin

    Static margin is the normalized distance between the neutral point and CG.

    💡

    SMstick fixed​=cˉxNP​​−cˉxCG​​

    Positive value indicates that CG is located forward of NP.
    Negative value indicates that     CG is located aft of NP.

    • For longitudinal static stability SM>0
    • Value of SM describes degree of stability

    Equation for static margin,

    💡

    SMstick fixed​=−CLαw​​​Cmα​​​≈−dCL​dCm​​

    The above equation assumes that CL​≈CLw​​.

    #Longitudinal Maneuvering Flight

    A maneuvering flight is where load factor n=WL​>1.

    Assumptions:

    • Aircraft is at the start of pull-up from steady level flight
    • Changes in angle of attack and load factor are small

    Effect of pitch rate on moments due to tail,

    Δαt​=Vqlt​​

    ΔCLt​​=CLαt​​​Δαt​=CLαt​​​Vqlt​​

    ΔMt​=−lt​ΔLt​=−lt​21​ρVt​2St​ΔCLt​​

    In non-dimensional form,

    ΔCmt​​=−ηH​VH​ΔCLt​​

    ΔCmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​Vqlt​​

    💡

    ΔCmt​​=−ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(n−1)V2glt​​

    where pull-up rate q=Vg(n−1)​

    #Maneuver Margin

    Total aircraft pitching moment during a maneuver,

    (CmCG​​)m​=Cm0​​+Cmα​​α+Cm​(q)

    (CmCG​​)m​=(CmCG​​)stick fixed​+Cm​(q)

    where the last term is contribution by the tail

    Tail contribution to maneuver margin,

    dCL​dCm​(q)​≈ΔCL​ΔCmCGt​​​​

    dCL​dCm​(q)​=−[(n−1)W]/[21​ρV2S]ηH​VH​CLαt​​​(n−1)V2glt​​​

    💡

    dCL​dCm​(q)​=−2μηH​VH​CLαt​​​​

    Relative density parameter is defined as,

    💡

    μ=ρglt​SW​

    Equation for maneuver margin,

    💡

    MM=SMstick fixed​+2μηH​VH​CLαt​​​​=−dCL​d(CmCG​​)m​​

    In general, maneuver margin is larger than static margin.

    #Maneuver Point

    The maneuver point is the location of the CG at which dCL​d(CmCG​​)m​​=0.

    The point at which moments about the CG during a maneuver is zero.

    Location of the maneuver point whereby MM=0,

    0=SMstick fixed​+2μηH​VH​CLαt​​​​

    0=cˉxNP​​−cˉxCG​​+2μηH​VH​CLαt​​​​

    💡

    cˉxMP​​=cˉxNP​​+2μηH​VH​CLαt​​​​

    Note that CLαt​​​ is expresed in per radian.
