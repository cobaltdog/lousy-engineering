    MA3703 · FLIGHT DYNAMICS

    Linearization of Aircraft EoM

    #Aircraft Dynamics Nomenclature

    #Body-Axis Velocity and Force Components

    Body-fixed velocities:

    • Axial velocity u
    • Lateral velocity v
    • Normal velocity w

    Body-fixed forces:

    • Axial force X
    • Side force Y
    • Normal force Z

    Rates:

    • Roll rate p
    • Pitch rate q
    • Yaw rate r

    #Angle of Attack and Sideslip

    Angle of attack is defined as,

    α=tan−1uw​≈uw​ (approximation if <15°)

    Sideslip angle is defined as,

    β=sin−1Vv​≈Vv​ (approximation if <15°)

    Velocity components,

    V=u2+v2+w2​

    u=Vcosβcosα

    v=Vsinβ

    w=Vcosβsinα

    #Stability Axis System

    Axis system:

    • Essentially the body-axis system rotated about lateral axis yb​ axis by angle −α0​
    • Normal component of flight velocity w=0
    • Axial component of flight velocity u=V

    #Decoupling Equations of Motion

    Equations of motion (forces),

    X−mgsinθ=m(u˙+qw−rv)

    Y+mgcosθsinϕ=m(v˙+ru−pw)

    Z+mgcosθcosϕ=m(w˙+pv−qu)

    Equations of motion (moments),

    L=Ixx​p˙​−Ixz​r˙+(Izz​−Iyy​)qr−Ixz​pq

    M=Iyy​q˙​+(Ixx​−Izz​)pr+Ixz​(p2−r2)

    N=−Ixz​p˙​+Izz​r˙+(Iyy​−Ixx​)pq+Ixz​qr

    Two distinct modes of aircraft motion:

    • Longitudinal motion — X, Z and M dependent on (u, w, q, u˙ ,w˙, δe​, δT​)
    • Lateral-directional motion — Y, L and N dependent on (v, p, r, v˙, δr​, δa​)

    #Linearization of Equations

    #Reference Flight Condition

    Assume reference flight condition to be a steady longitudinal flight (with no rotation).

    • Steady level flight — flight path angle = 0
    • Steady climb flight — flight path angle > 0
    • Steady descent flight — flight path angle < 0

    Steady flight conditions:

    • Axial velocity u0​= constant
    • Normal velocity w0​=0
    • Aircraft pitch angle θ0​= constant

    Longitudinal flight (with no rotation):

    • Lateral velocity v0​=0
    • Rates p0​=q0​=r0​=0
    • Euler rates ψ0​=ϕ0​=0

    Note that the stability axes are fixed to the body and treated as a body-fixed axis
    system such that we can make use of the equations derived in the modelling of
    the aircraft. The     xs​-axis coincides with the aircraft velocity vector V such that
    there is no normal component. Thus,     w0​=0.

    Consider a small disturbance in angle of attack due to incremental normal velocity Δw.

    💡

    α=α0​+Δα

    💡

    θ=θ0​−Δα

    Consider a small disturbance in angle of attack due to incremental pitch angle Δθ.

    💡

    α=α0​+Δα

    💡

    θ=θ0​+Δθ

    #Linearizing the Equations

    Axial force, due to disturbance, is given by,

    X−mgsinθ=m(u˙+qw−rv)

    At equilibrium, where disturbance is zero,

    X0​−mgsinθ0​=0

    Consider a small disturbance from the reference flight condition.

    X=X0​+ΔX

    θ=θ0​+Δθ

    u=u0​+Δu

    u˙=u˙0​+Δu˙=Δu˙

    v=v0​+Δv=Δv

    w=w0​+Δw=Δw

    q=q0​+Δq=Δq

    r=r0​+Δr=Δr

    Substituting into the equation,

    (X0​+ΔX)−mgsin(θ0​+Δθ)=m(Δu˙+ΔqΔw−ΔrΔv)

    (X0​+ΔX)−mg(sinθ0​+cosθ0​⋅Δθ)=mΔu˙

    Expansion using trigonometric identity for small Δθ.
    Product of two small quantities are negligible.

    Substituting the equilibrium equation,

    (X0​−mgsinθ0​)+ΔX−mg(cosθ0​⋅Δθ)=mΔu˙

    ΔX−mg(cosθ0​)Δθ=mΔu˙

    Linearized equations for longitudinal motion,

    💡

    ΔX−mg(cosθ0​)Δθ=mΔu˙

    ΔZ−mg(sinθ0​)Δθ=m(Δw˙−u0​Δq)

    ΔM=Iyy​Δq˙​

    Linearized equations for lateral-directional motion,

    💡

    ΔY+mg(cosθ0​)Δϕ=m(Δv˙+u0​Δr)

    ΔL=Ixx​Δp˙​−Ixz​Δr˙

    ΔN=−Ixz​Δp˙​+Izz​Δr˙

    #Further Simplification

    Stability derivatives w.r.t. motion,

    X(_)​=m1​∂(_)∂X​

    Y(_)​=m1​∂(_)∂Y​

    Z(_)​=m1​∂(_)∂Z​

    Control derivatives w.r.t. control,

    L(_)​=Ixx​1​∂(_)∂L​

    M(_)​=Iyy​1​∂(_)∂M​

    N(_)​=Izz​1​∂(_)∂N​

    where (_) refer to the variables u, v, w or δe​, δr​, δa​, δT​ etc.

    Using Taylor series expansion,

    ΔX=∂u∂X​Δu+∂w∂X​Δw+∂δe​∂X​Δδe​+∂δT​∂X​ΔδT​

    mΔX​=m1​(∂u∂X​Δu+∂w∂X​Δw+∂δe​∂X​Δδe​+∂δT​∂X​ΔδT​)

    mΔX​=Xu​Δu+Xw​Δw+Xδe​​Δδe​+XδT​​ΔδT​

    Linearized axial force equation,

    mΔu˙=ΔX−mg(cosθ0​)Δθ

    Δu˙=mΔX​−g(cosθ0​)Δθ

    Δu˙=Xu​Δu+Xw​Δw+Xδe​​Δδe​+XδT​​ΔδT​−g(cosθ0​)Δθ

    Linearized equations for longitudinal motion,

    💡

    Δu˙=Xu​Δu+Xw​Δw+Xδe​​Δδe​+XδT​​ΔδT​−g(cosθ0​)Δθ

    💡

    (1−Zw˙​)Δw˙=Zu​Δu+Zw​Δw+(Zq​+u0​)Δq                           +Zδe​​Δδe​+ZδT​​ΔδT​−g(sinθ0​)Δθ

    💡

    Δq˙​=Mu​Δu+Mw​Δw+Mw˙​Δw˙+Mq​Δq+Mδe​​Δδe​+MδT​​ΔδT​

    Linearized equations for lateral-directional motion,

    💡

    Δv˙=Yv​Δv+Yp​Δp+(Yr​−u0​)Δr+Yδr​​Δδr​+g(cosθ0​)Δϕ

    💡

    Δp˙​−Ixx​Ixz​​Δr˙=Lv​Δv+Lp​Δp+Lr​Δr+Lδa​​Δδa​+Lδr​​Δδr​

    💡

    Δr˙−Izz​Ixz​​Δp˙​=Nv​Δv+Np​Δp+Nr​Δr+Nδa​​Δδa​+Nδr​​Δδr​
