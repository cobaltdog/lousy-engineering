    #2-DOF System

    #Free Vibration 2-DOF

    Equations of motion,

    [m0​0m​]{x¨1​x¨2​​}+[2k−k​−k2k​]{x1​x2​​}={00​}

    [M​]{X¨​}+[K​]{X​}={0​}
    1st matrix represents the mass matrix.
    2nd matrix represents the stiffness matrix.

    Assume free vibration response to be,

    x1​=A1​sin(ωt)     →     x¨1​=−ω2A1​sin(ωt)

    x2​=A2​sin(ωt)     →     x¨2​=−ω2A2​sin(ωt)

    For a given problem, A1​, A2​, and ω are to be determined.

    Equations of motion becomes,

    [2k−ω2m−k​−k2k−ω2m​]{A1​A2​​}={00​}

    Natural frequency and mode shapes:

    • Equate determinant of matrix to zero and solve for natural frequencies
    • Substitute natural frequencies into equations of motion
    • Let A1​=1 or A2​=1 to obtain the mode shapes

    Vibration response of each mode shape,

    {x1​x2​​}1​=C1​ϕ1​sin(ω1​t+α1​)

    {x1​x2​​}2​=C2​ϕ2​sin(ω2​t+α2​)

    Unknowns C1​, C2​, α1​, and α2​ are to be
    determined from given initial conditions.

    Total vibration response,

    {x​}={x​}1​+{x​}2​

    {x1​x2​​}=C1​ϕ1​sin(ω1​t+α1​)+C2​ϕ2​sin(ω2​t+α2​)

    {x˙1​x˙2​​}=C1​ω1​ϕ1​cos(ω1​t+α1​)+C2​ω2​ϕ2​cos(ω2​t+α2​)

    #Forced Vibration 2-DOF

    Equations of motion,

    [m1​0​0m2​​]{x¨1​x¨2​​}+[k1​+k2​−k2​​−k2​k2​+k3​​]{x1​x2​​}={F1​sin(ωt)F2​sin(ωt)​}

    [M​]{X¨​}+[K​]{X​}={0​}
    1st matrix represents the mass matrix.
    2nd matrix represents the stiffness matrix.

    If forced vibration is Fi​sin(ωt), vibration response is,

    x1​=X1​sin(ωt)   →   x¨1​=−ω2X1​sin(ωt)

    x2​=X2​sin(ωt)   →   x¨2​=−ω2X2​sin(ωt)

    whereby excitation frequency ω is known,
    and amplitudes     X1​ and X2​ are unknowns

    Equations of motion becomes,

    [k1​+k2​−m1​ω2−k2​​−k2​k2​+k3​−m2​ω2​]{X1​X2​​}={F1​F2​​}

    Note that if the excitation frequency ω is not equal to any of the system’s
    natural frequencies, the determinant of the matrix is not zero,     det∣A∣=0.

    Cramer’s rule,

    Δ1​=det​F1​F2​​−k2​k2​+k3​−m2​ω2​​

    Δ2​=det​k1​+k2​−m1​ω2−k2​​F1​F2​​​

    Amplitudes of vibration response of each mass,

    X1​=ΔΔ1​​

    X2​=ΔΔ2​​
