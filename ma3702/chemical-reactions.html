    #Heat of Reaction (HOR)

    Dry air can be approximated as 21% oxygen and 79% nitrogen by mole numbers.

    💡
    1 kmol O2​+3.76 kmol N2​=4.76 kmol Air

    Energy balance for combustion process,

    Q=Hproducts​−Hreactants​=ΔH

    Heat of reaction at the reference state of T=25°C and P=1 bar.

    QHOR​=∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)p​−∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)r​

    QHOR​=∑N(hˉf°​)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​

    where hˉf°​ is the enthalpy of formation

    Heat of reaction at states other than the reference state.

    QHOR​=∑mi​(hˉf,i°​+∫Tref​Tp​​ cp,i​ dT)p​+∑mi​(hˉf,i°​+∫Tref​Tp​​ cp,i​ dT)r​

    QHOR​=∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)p​−∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)r​

    where hˉ is the sensible enthalpy at given temperature,
    and     hˉ° is the sensible enthalpy at reference state

    Heat of reaction:

    • QHOR​<0, exothermic reaction — system releases heat (may proceed unaided)
    • QHOR​>0, endothermic reaction — system absorbs heat (require inputs to proceed)

    Heating value of a fuel,

    💡

    qHV​=−Mfuel​QHOR​​

    where Mfuel​ is the molar mass of the fuel

    #Adiabatic Flame Temperature

    In the absence of work interactions, chemical energy released during combustion is either lost as heat to
    the surroundings or used internally to raise the temperature of the products.

    Assuming no heat loss, temperature of the products reaches a maximum — adiabatic flame temperature.

    • Guides cooling criteria of combustor liner
    • Affects engine sizing when flow enters turbine

    Assuming no heat loss,

    0=Hproducts​−Hreactants​

    0=∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)p​−∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)r​

    If the reactants are at the reference state such that hˉr​=hˉr°​,

    0=∑N(hˉf°​+hˉad​−hˉ°)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​

    ∑N(hˉ°−hˉad​)p​=∑N(hˉf°​)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​

    ∑N(hˉad​−hˉ°)p​=−[∑N(hˉf°​)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​]

    where hˉad​ is the sensible enthalpy at adiabatic flame temperature

    Adiabatic flame temperature,

    💡

    ∑mi​(∫Tref​Tad​​ cp,i​ dT)p​=−[∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)p​−∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)r​]

    Stoichiometric ratio is the proportion of fuel-to-oxidizer that
    will produce no excess reactants and only stable products.

    💡

    fst​=moxid​mfuel​​

    Equivalence ratio is the proportion of actual to stoichiometric fuel-to-oxidizer ratio.

    💡

    ϕ=fst​f​

    If ϕ<1, the mixture of reactants is fuel lean.
    If     ϕ>1, the mixture of reactants is fuel rich.
