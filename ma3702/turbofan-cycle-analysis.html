    Turbofan Cycle Analysis

    #Turbofan (Separate Exhaust)

    StationProcess
    a→2Diffuser compression
    2→8Fan compression
    8→3Compressor
    8→efFan nozzle
    3→4Combustor
    4→5Turbine
    5→7Core Nozzle

    Bypass ratio is defined as,

    💡

    B=m˙a​m˙B​​

    where m˙a​ is the core flow,
    and     m˙B​ is the bypass flow

    #Inlet (a→2)

    Temperature and pressure ratios across the inlet,

    💡

    Ta​T2​​=1+2cpa​​Ta​Ua​2​

    💡

    Pa​P2​​=(1+ηi​2cpa​​Ta​Ua​2​)γ−1γ​=(1+ηi​2γ−1​Ma​2)γ−1γ​

    Note that the equations are the same as that of a turbojet.

    #Fan (2→8)

    Temperature and pressure ratios across the fan (same as compressor),

    💡

    T2​T8​​=1+ηf​1​[(P2​P8​​)γγ−1​−1]

    💡

    P2​P8​​=[1+ηf​(T2​T8​​−1)]γ−1γ​

    where fan pressure ratio Prf​=P8​/P2​, and ηf​ is the fan isentropic efficiency

    #Fan Nozzle (8→ef)

    Fan nozzle exit velocity,

    T8​=Tef​+2cpa​​Uef​2​

    Uef​=2cpa​​(T8​−Tef​)​

    Uef​=2cpa​​ηfn​T8​[1−(P8​Pef​​)γγ−1​]​

    💡

    Uef​=2ηfn​γ−1γ​Ra​T8​[1−(P8​Pef​​)γγ−1​]​

    Note that the equation is the same as the core nozzle.

    Temperature and pressure ratios across the fan nozzle (same as core nozzle),

    💡

    T8​Tef​​=1−ηfn​[1−(P8​Pef​​)γγ−1​]

    💡

    P8​Pef​​=[1−ηfn​1​(1−T8​Tef​​)]γ−1γ​

    where ηfn​ is the fan nozzle isentropic efficiency

    Fan nozzle may be choked and must be checked for this condition.

    • Determine the fan thrust based on unchoked/choked nozzle
    • Choking less likely to occur as nozzle pressure ratio is small
    • Typically Pef​=Pa​

    #Turbine (4→5)

    Temperature and pressure ratios across the turbine,

    💡

    T4​T5​​=1−ηt​[1−(P4​P5​​)γγ−1​]

    💡

    P4​P5​​=[1−ηt​1​(1−T4​T5​​)]γ−1γ​

    Note that the equations are the same as that of a turbojet.

    #Fan-Compressor-Turbine Work

    Mechanical work input to fan and compressor
    is equal to mechanical work output of turbine.

    W˙m,f​+W˙m,c​=W˙m,t​

    ηm,f​W˙f​​+ηm,c​W˙c​​=W˙t​ηm,t​

    Excluding transmission efficiency.

    Neglecting all mechanical inefficiencies,

    W˙f​+W˙c​=W˙t​

    Expressing the work in terms of bypass and core flow,

    W˙bypass​+W˙core​=W˙t​

    Bm˙a​cpa​​(T8​−T2​)+m˙a​cpa​​(T3​−T2​)=(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​(T4​−T5​)

    #Core Nozzle (5→7)

    Core nozzle exit velocity,

    T5​=T7​+2cpg​​U7​2​

    U7​=2cpg​​(T5​−T7​)​

    U7​=2cpg​​ηn​T5​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    💡

    U7​=2ηn​γ−1γ​Rg​T5​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    Note that the equation is the same as that of a turbojet.

    Temperature and pressure ratios across the core nozzle,

    💡

    T5​T7​​=1−ηn​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]

    💡

    P5​P7​​=[1−ηn​1​(1−T5​T7​​)]γ−1γ​

    Note that the equations are the same as that of a turbojet.

    #Engine Thrust and Fuel Consumption

    Thrust produced by the engine,

    T=∑ Momentum Flux+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=(∑out​ m˙U−∑in​ m˙U)+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=[m˙a​(1+f)Ue​+Bm˙a​Uef​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​]         +[(Pe​−Pa​)Ae​+(Pef​−Pa​)Aef​]

    If freestream pressure is equal to fan and core exhaust pressure,

    💡

    T=m˙a​(1+f)Ue​+Bm˙a​Uef​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​

    Specific thrust based on core flow,

    💡

    m˙a​T​=(1+f)Ue​+BUef​−(1+B)Ua​

    Specific thrust based on total flow,

    💡

    (1+B)m˙a​T​=1+B(1+f)Ue​+BUef​​−Ua​

    Thrust specific fuel consumption,

    💡

    TSFC=Tm˙f​​=(1+f)Ue​+BUef​−(1+B)Ua​f​

    #Turbofan (Mixed Exhaust)

    Fan flow is mixed with the core flow before expanding to a single nozzle.

    Advantages of mixed exhaust:

    • Small increase in thrust
    • Lower exhaust velocity results in reduced jet noise
    • Lower exhaust gas temperature (better stealth capabilities)

    Disadvantages of mixed exhaust:

    • Longer engine casing to channel fan flow to core nozzle (increase in weight)
    • Full mixing is not likely to occur, more difficult to analyze performance

    Assuming adiabatic mixing with no pressure losses,

    Ein​=Eout​

    (1+f)m˙a​cp​T5​+Bm˙a​cp​T8​=(1+f+B)m˙a​cp​Tmix​

    Mixing temperature, assuming cp​ to be constant,

    💡

    Tmix​=1+f+B(1+f)T5​+BT8​​

    Nozzle exit velocity,

    Tmix​=T7​+2cp​U7​2​

    U7​=2cp​(Tmix​−T7​)​

    U7​=2cp​ηn​Tmix​[1−(Pmix​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    💡

    U7​=2ηn​γ−1γ​RTmix​[1−(Pmix​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    Note that the equation is the same as that of a turbojet.

    #Engine Thrust and Fuel Consumption

    Thrust produced by the engine,

    T=∑ Momentum Flux+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=(∑out​ m˙U−∑in​ m˙U)+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=[m˙a​(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​]+(Pe​−Pa​)Ae​

    If freestream pressure is equal to exhaust pressure,

    💡

    T=m˙a​(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​

    Specific thrust based on core flow,

    💡

    m˙a​T​=(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)Ua​

    Specific thrust based on total flow,

    💡

    (1+B)m˙a​T​=1+B(1+f+B)Ue​​−Ua​

    Thrust specific fuel consumption,

    💡

    TSFC=Tm˙f​​=(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)Ua​f​

    #Turbofan (Aft Mounted)

    StationProcess
    a→2Diffuser compression
    2→3Compressor
    3→4Combustor
    4→5Turbine
    5→6Fan turbine
    6→7Core nozzle
    2→8Fan compression
    8→efFan nozzle

    Aft-mounted fan:

    • Fan inlet has the same properties as compressor inlet
    • Temperature and pressure ratios are the same as that for a compressor
    • Fan turbine is driven by core flow (work output of fan turbine = work input to fan)
    • Total thrust produced is the sum of fan nozzle thrust Tfan​ and core nozzle thrust Tcore​
