    #Engine Performance

    Stagnation (total) temperature,

    T0​=T+2cp​U2​

    where T0​ is the total temperature,
    and     T is the static temperature

    General thrust equation,

    T=m˙a​[(1+f)Ue​−Ua​]+(Pe​−Pa​)Ae​

    where fuel-air ratio f=m˙a​m˙f​​

    Simplified thrust equation,

    T≈m˙a​(Ue​−Ua​)

    Assuming f≪1 and Pe​=Pa​.

    Thermal Efficiency

    General equation,

    ηth​=Q˙​fuel​ΔKEpropellent​​=m˙f​QR​21​m˙a​[(1+f)Ue​2−Ua​2]​

    where QR​ is the calorific value of the fuel

    Based on simplified thrust,

    ηth​≈2fQR​Ue​2−Ua​2​

    Valid only for f≪1 and Pe​=Pa​.

    Propulsion Efficiency

    General equation,

    ηp​=ΔKEpropellent​Thrust Power​=21​m˙a​[(1+f)Ue​2−Ua​2]TUa​​

    For a single propellent stream.

    Based on simplified thrust,

    ηp​≈Ue​+Ua​2Ua​​=1+Ue​/Ua​2​

    Valid only for f≪1 and Pe​=Pa​.

    Overall Efficiency

    General equation,

    ηo​=Fuel PowerThrust Power​=m˙f​QR​TUa​​=ηp​×ηth​

    Based on simplified thrust,

    ηo​≈fQR​(Ue​−Ua​)Ua​​

    Unused Kinetic Energy

    Unused KE=21​m˙a​(ue​−ua​)2

    Valid only for f≪1 and Pe​=Pa​.

    Specific Thrust

    Ts​=m˙a​T​

    Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption

    TSFC=Tm˙f​​=ηo​QR​Ua​​

    #Diffuser (a→2)

    Temperature ratio,

    Ta​T2​​=1+2cpa​​Ta​Ua​2​

    Ta​T2s​​=1+ηi​2cpa​​Ta​Ua​2​=1+ηi​2γ−1​Ma​2

    where ηi​ is the inlet isentropic efficiency

    Pressure ratio,

    Pa​P2​​=Pa​P2s​​=(Ta​T2s​​)γ−1γ​

    Pa​P2​​=(1+ηi​2cpa​​Ta​Ua​2​)γ−1γ​=(1+ηi​2γ−1​Ma​2)γ−1γ​

    where ηi​ is the inlet isentropic efficiency

    #Compressor (2→3)

    Temperature ratio,

    T2​T3​​=1+ηc​1​[(P2​P3​​)γγ−1​−1]

    T2​T3s​​=1+ηc​(T2​T3​​−1)

    where ηc​ is the compressor isentropic efficiency

    Pressure ratio,

    P2​P3​​=[1+ηc​(T2​T3​​−1)]γ−1γ​

    where ηc​ is the compressor isentropic efficiency

    Polytropic Compression Process

    P2​P3​​=(T2​T3​​)n−1n​

    ηc,poly​=n−1n​⋅γγ−1​

    Compressor Work

    Mechanical work input to compressor,

    W˙m,c​=ηm,c​m˙a​cpa​​(T3​−T2​)​

    where ηm,c​ is the mechanical efficiency

    #Combustor (3→4)

    Heat addition into combustor,

    Qin​=(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​T4​−m˙a​cpa​​T3​

    Combustion efficiency,

    ηb​=Q˙​fuel​ΔH˙​=m˙f​QR​(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​T4​−m˙a​cpa​​T3​​

    #Turbine (4→5)

    Temperature ratio,

    T4​T5​​=1−ηt​[1−(P4​P5​​)γγ−1​]

    T4​T5s​​=1−ηt​1​(1−T4​T5​​)

    where ηt​ is the turbine isentropic efficiency

    Pressure ratio,

    P4​P5​​=[1−ηt​1​(1−T4​T5​​)]γ−1γ​

    where ηt​ is the turbine isentropic efficiency

    Polytropic Expansion Process

    P4​P5​​=(T4​T5​​)n−1n​

    ηt,poly​=nn−1​⋅γ−1γ​

    Compressor-Turbine Work

    Mechanical work output of turbine,

    W˙m,t​=[(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​(T4​−T5​)]ηm,t​

    where ηm,t​ is the mechanical efficiency

    Accounting for transmission efficiency,

    W˙in​=W˙out​

    W˙m,c​=W˙m,t​

    ηm,c​m˙a​cpa​​(T3​−T2​)​=ηtrans​[(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​(T4​−T5​)]ηm,t​

    where ηtrans​ is the transmission efficiency of the engine spool

    #Exhaust Nozzle (5→7)

    Temperature ratio,

    T5​T7​​=1−ηn​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]

    T5​T7s​​=1−ηn​1​(1−T5​T7​​)

    where ηn​ is the nozzle isentropic efficiency

    Pressure ratio,

    P5​P7​​=[1−ηn​1​(1−T5​T7​​)]γ−1γ​=[1−ηn​1​(1−1+2γ−1​M7​21​)]γ−1γ​

    where ηn​ is the nozzle isentropic efficiency

    Choked or Unchoked Condition

    Critical pressure of nozzle,

    P5​Pcr​​=(1−ηn​1​γ+1γ−1​)γ−1γ​

    If Pcr​>Pa​ nozzle is choked.
    If     Pcr​<Pa​ nozzle is unchoked.

    Nozzle Exit Velocity

    U7​=2cpg​​(T5​−T7​)​

    U7​=2cpg​​ηn​T5​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    U7​=2ηn​γ−1γ​Rg​T5​[1−(P5​P7​​)γγ−1​]​

    Expressing as Mach number,

    M7​=γ−12​(T7​T5​​−1)​

    #Turbofan Cycle

    Bypass ratio,

    B=m˙a​m˙B​​

    where m˙B​ is the bypass flow, and m˙a​ is the core flow

    #Separate Exhaust

    Station
    a→1Freestream air

    2→8Fan

    8→efFan nozzle

    4→5Turbine

    Fan-Compressor-Turbine Work

    W˙f​+W˙c​=W˙t​

    W˙bypass​+W˙core​=W˙t​

    Bm˙a​cpa​​(T8​−T2​)+m˙a​cpa​​(T3​−T2​)=(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​(T4​−T5​)

    Bcpa​​(T8​−T2​)+cpa​​(T3​−T2​)=(1+f)cpg​​(T4​−T5​)

    Thrust Equation

    General equation,

    T=(∑out​ m˙U−∑in​ m˙U)+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=[m˙a​(1+f)Ue​+Bm˙a​Uef​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​]         +[(Pe​−Pa​)Ae​+(Pef​−Pa​)Aef​]

    Assuming exhaust expands to ambient pressure,

    T=m˙a​(1+f)Ue​+Bm˙a​Uef​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​

    Engine Performance

    Specific thrust (based on core flow),

    m˙a​T​=(1+f)Ue​+BUef​−(1+B)Ua​

    Specific thrust (based on total flow),

    (1+B)m˙a​T​=1+B(1+f)Ue​+BUef​​−Ua​

    Thrust specific fuel consumption,

    TSFC=Tm˙f​​=m˙a​m˙f​​⋅Tm˙a​​=(1+f)Ue​+BUef​−(1+B)Ua​f​

    8→mixMixing chamber
    3→4Combustor
    4→5Turbine

    Mixing Temperature

    Energy balance of mixing chamber,

    E˙in​=E˙out​

    E˙bypass​+E˙core​=E˙mix​

    Bm˙a​cpa​​T8​+(m˙a​+m˙f​)cpg​​T5​=(m˙a​+m˙f​+m˙B​)cpmix​​Tmix​

    Bcpa​​T8​+(1+f)cpg​​T5​=(1+f+B)cpmix​​Tmix​

    Tmix​=1+f+BBT8​+(1+f)T5​​

    Assuming specific heats to be constant.

    Thrust Equation

    General equation,

    T=(∑out​ m˙U−∑in​ m˙U)+∑ Pressure Thrust

    T=[m˙a​(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​]+(Pe​−Pa​)Ae​

    Assuming exhaust expands to ambient pressure,

    T=m˙a​(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)m˙a​Ua​

    Engine Performance

    Specific thrust (based on core flow),

    m˙a​T​=(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)Ua​

    Specific thrust (based on total flow),

    (1+B)m˙a​T​=1+B(1+f+B)Ue​​−Ua​

    Thrust specific fuel consumption,

    TSFC=Tm˙f​​=m˙a​m˙f​​⋅Tm˙a​​=(1+f+B)Ue​−(1+B)Ua​f​

    #Axial Compressors

    Static temperature and pressure,

    P1​P0,1​​=(T1​T0,1​​)γ−1γ​ (isentropic relations)

    T0,1​=T1​+2cp​C1​2​ (stagnation concept)

    Euler’s equations,

    W˙=m˙Δh

    W˙comp​=m˙(U2​Cθ,2​−U1​Cθ,1​)

    W˙turb​=m˙(U1​Cθ,1​−U2​Cθ,2​)

    For a normal stage, the following applies,

    • U=rω (rotor speed is constant based on mean radius)
    • Cz​=const. (axial velocity of flow is constant throughout)
    • C1​=C3​ (rotor inlet absolute velocity = stator outlet absolute velocity)
    • α1​=α3​ (rotor inlet flow absolute angle = stator outlet flow absolute angle)

    Relation between velocities,

    C=U+w

    U=Cθ​+wθ​

    #Total Enthalpy Change

    Total enthalpy change across compressor stage,

    m˙W˙​=h0,3​−h0,1​=cp​(T0,3​−T0,1​)

    Total enthalpy change across compressor stage,

    m˙W˙​=UCz​(tanα2​−tanα1​)

    m˙W˙​=U(U−Cz​tanβ2​−Cz​tanα1​)

    m˙W˙​=UCz​(tanβ1​−tanβ2​)

    α1​ → Rotor inlet flow absolute angle
    β1​ → Rotor inlet flow relative angle
    α2​ → Rotor outlet flow absolute angle
    β2​ → Rotor outlet flow relative angle

    Angles are measured from the axial axis.

    #Stage Pressure Rise

    Total pressure ratio across compressor stage,

    rstage​=P0,1​P0,3​​=(1+ηst​T0,1​ΔT0stage​​​)γ−1γ​

    rstage​=P0,1​P0,3​​=(1+ηst​cp​T0,1​UΔCθ​​)γ−1γ​

    #Degree of Reaction

    Degree of reaction (general equation),

    R=Δhstage​Δhrotor​​=Δhrotor​+Δhstator​Δhrotor​​

    Degree of reaction (isentropic & incompressible flow),

    R=ΔPstator​ΔProtor​​

    Equation in terms of the different velocities,

    R=Δhstage​Δhrotor​​=h3​−h1​h2​−h1​​=2U(Cθ,2​−Cθ,1​)w1​2−w2​2​

    R=2Uwθ,1​+wθ,2​​ (if Cz​ and U are constant)

    Equation in terms of the different flow angles,

    R=21​ϕ(tanβ1​+tanβ2​)

    R=1−21​ϕ(tanα1​+tanα2​)

    R=21​[1−ϕ(tanα1​−tanβ2​)]

    where flow coefficient ϕ=UCz​​

    #Non-Dimensional Parameters

    Stage loading coefficient,

    ψ=U2Δh0stage​​​=U2cp​ΔT0stage​​​

    Coefficient of total pressure,

    δ=Pref​P0​​=101325P0​​

    Coefficient of rotor speed,

    θ1​​N1​​=θ1′​​N1′​​

    Coefficient of total temperature,

    θ=Tref​T0​​=288T0​​

    Coefficient of mass flow rate,

    δ1​m˙1​θ1​​​=δ1′​m˙1′​θ1′​​​

    where subscript 1 represents the designed value,
    and subscript 1’ represents the actual value

    #Chemical Reactions

    Chemical composition of dry air,

    1 kmol O2​+3.76 kmol N2​=4.76 kmol Air

    Heat of reaction,

    Q=Hproducts​−Hreactants​

    QHOR​=∑N(hˉf°​)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​

    QHOR​=∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)p​−∑mi​(hˉf,i°​)r​

    where hˉf°​ is the enthalpy of formation at the reference state

    Heating value of a fuel,

    qHV​=−Mfuel​QHOR​​

    where Mfuel​ is the molar mass of the fuel

    Adiabatic flame temperature,

    0=Hproducts​−Hreactants​

    0=∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)p​−∑N(hˉf°​+hˉ−hˉ°)r​

    0=[∑N(hˉf°​)p​−∑N(hˉf°​)r​]+∑N(hˉ−hˉ°)p​

    0=QHOR​+∑N(hˉad​−hˉ°)p​

    ∑N(hˉad​−hˉ°)p​=−QHOR​

    ∑Ncp​(Tad​−Tref​)p​=−QHOR​

    Equivalence ratio,

    ϕ=fst​f​=(mf​/mo​)st​mf​/mo​​=(Nf​/No​)st​Nf​/No​​=Nf,st​No​Nf​No,st​​

    where fst​ is the stoichiometric ratio
