    #Question 2

    Write a program to compute the solutions for x and y in the linear system of equations.

    a1​x+b1​y=c1​

    a2​x+b2​y=c2​

    The solutions for x and y are given by,

    x=a1​b2​−a2​b1​b2​c1​−b1​c2​​

    y=a1​b2​−a2​b1​a1​c2​−a2​c1​​

    Assume that the denominator is always non-zero. The program reads
    in     a1​, b1​, c1​, a2​, b2​ and c2​, and computes and prints out the solutions.

    The distance of a marathon is 42.2km. A runner passes himself such that:

    (i) his average speed for the first 12km is 15km/h

    (ii) He reduces the exertion for the next 15km with an average speed of 10km/h

    (iii) He increases his speed to 14km/h for the next 10km.

    (iv) For the remaining distance, he makes a dash for it by averaging 18km/h.

    Write a program to calculate and print the time he took to complete the marathon.

    An encryption system uses the following rules:

    • A letter, uppercase or lowercase, is replaced with a letter of the same case five
      places up the alphabet. If this falls pass the end of the alphabets, it is wrapped
      round to the beginning. So “b” becomes “g”, and “y” becomes “d”.
    • A digit is replaced by a digit larger by 3. If this is greater than 9, it is wrapped
      round to the other end. So 3 becomes 6, and 8 becomes 1.
    • All other characters remain unchanged.
