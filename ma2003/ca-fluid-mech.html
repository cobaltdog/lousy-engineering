    Find the magnitude of the force acting on the right-side surface of the block, Fright​.

    Ptop​=P−ρg2dz​

    Pbtm​=P+ρg2dz​

    Fright​=Ptop​dz+21​(Pbtm​−Ptop​)dz

    Fright​=(P−21​ρg dz)dz+21​(ρg dz)dz

    Fright​=P dz

    Find the magnitude of the force acting on the bottom surface of the block, Fbtm​.

    Fbtm​=Pbtm​ dy

    Fbtm​=(P+21​ρg dz)dy

    Find the magnitude of the force acting on the top surface of the block, Ftop​.

    Ftop​=Ptop​ dy

    Ftop​=(P−21​ρg dz)dy

    Determine ∂z∂P​ using Newton’s second law in the z-direction ∑Fz​=maz​.

    Fbtm​−Ftop​−W=maz​

    #Question 2

    The pressure on the infinitesimal segment ds is:

    The magnitude of the force exerted on the segement ds is:

    P=P0​+ρgh

    F=(P0​+ρgh)ds

    The magnitude of the horizontal force exerted on the infinitesimal segment ds is:

    The magnitude of the vertical force exerted on the infinitesimal segment ds is:

    Fh​=(P0​+ρgh)ds⋅sinθ

    Fv​=(P0​+ρgh)ds⋅cosθ

    Discuss how the horizontal force is a projected area problem and
    the vertical force is the weight of the volume of fluid above the area.

    ds⋅sinθ=Height

    Height×Width=Area

    Fh​=(P0​+ρgh)Aprojected​

    Thus, the horizontal force acts on the projected vertical area of the gate.

    ds⋅cosθ=Length

    Length×Height×Width=Volume

    Fv​=P0​(ds⋅cosθ)+ρg(h ds⋅cosθ)

    Fv​=P0​Aprojected​+ρgVˉfluid​

    Fv​=P0​Aprojected​+mfluid​g

    Fv​=P0​Aprojected​+Wfluid​

    Thus, the vertical force is equal to the weight of the volume of fluid above.

    #Part (b)

    yh1​=21​(2)=1 m (from the pivot)

    yh2​=32​(2)=34​ m (from the pivot)

    yair​=21​(2)=1 m (from the pivot)

    119381yh​=82404(1)+39240(34​)−2262.7(1)

    yh​=1.1096 m (from the pivot)

    yh​=1.1096+3=4.1096 m (from the free surface)

    #Part (c)

    The net vertical force due to fluid 2 is the weight of the triangle.

    The compressed air also exerts a vertical forces on the gate.

    Fv,f2​=1000(9.81)⋅21​(2×2)(2)

    Fv,f2​=39240 N (acting upwards)

    Fv,air​=800cos45°(2×2)−800(2×2)

    Fv,air​=−937.26 N (acting downwards)

    Fv,net​=39240−937.26

    Fv,net​=38303 N (acting upwards)

    #Part (d)

    xv,f2​=31​(2)=32​ m (from the pivot)

    xv,air​=21​(2)=1 m (from the pivot)

    38303xv​=39240(32​)−937.26(1)

    xv​=0.65851 m (from the pivot)

    #Part (e)

    Taking moments about the pivot, ∑Mpivot​=0.

    0=Mpivot​+119381(1.1096)−38303(0.65851)

    Mpivot​=−107241 Nm

    The negative sign indicates that a clockwise moment acts on the pivot.

    #Question 2

    #Part (a)

    Option C

    Hydrostatic pressure on the lower semicircle section of AB cancels each other out.

    #Part (b)

    Option A

    The vertical force acting on the left side of AB is equal to the weight of the volume
    of fluid above that surface. For the right side of AB, the force can be found by
    subtracting the weight of the volume of fluid acting on the upper region from
    the weight of the volume of fluid acting on the lower region.
