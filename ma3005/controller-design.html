    Controller Design

    #Basic Controllers

    Types of controllers:

    • Proportional-derivative (PD) controller
    • Proportional-integral (PI) controller
    • Prop-deri-int (PID) controller

    A PD controller improves the transient response and stability of a control system.
    A PI controller eliminates the steady-state error of a control system.

    Expression for PD controller,

    KP​+KD​s=KD​(s+KD​KP​​)

    KP​+KD​s=KD​(s+zc​)

    Expression for PI controller,

    KP​+sKI​​=KP​(ss+KI​/KP​​)

    KP​+sKI​​=KP​(ss+zc​​)

    Expression for PID controller,

    KP​+KD​s+sKI​​=sKD​s2+KP​s+KI​​

    KP​+KD​s+sKI​​=KD​(s+z1​)(ss+z2​​)

    #Improving Transient Response

    #PD Controller

    Design process of controller:

    • Determine the desired closed-loop poles
    • Obtain characteristic equation of closed-loop transfer function
    • Substitute the desired poles into the characteristic equation
    • Solve for KP​ and KD​ by comparing real and imaginary parts

    Suppose the desired response of the control system is:

    • 10% overshoot
    • 2% settling time of 0.5 seconds

    To achieve a 10% overshoot,

    Mp​=e−(ζπ/1−ζ2​)

    0.1=e−(ζπ/1−ζ2​)

    ln0.1=−1−ζ2​ζπ​

    ζ=0.59

    To achieve a settling time of 0.5 seconds,

    ts​=ζωn​4​

    0.5=0.59ωn​4​

    ωn​=13.5

    s1,2​=−ζωn​±ωn​ζ2−1​

    s1,2​=−8±j11

    #Lead Compensator

    Design process of controller:

    • Design the desired transient response with PD controller
    • Based on PD controller, specify a value of zc​ such that ∣zc​∣<∣zc′​∣
    • Obtain characteristic equation of the closed-loop transfer function
    • Substitute desired poles into the equation and solve for K and pc​

    From the design of PD controller,

    PD=14(s+13.1)

    PD=KD​(s+zc′​)

    Desired poles of control system,

    s1,2​=−8±j11

    Let zc​=10 such that zc​<zc′​.

    Lead=K(s+pc​s+10​)

    Characteristic equation of transfer function,

    0=1+G(s)⋅H(s)

    0=1+K(s+pc​s+10​)(s2+2s+21​)

    0=(s+pc​)(s2+2s+2)+K(s+10)

    0=s3+(2+pc​)s2+(2+2pc​+K)s+(10K+2pc​)

    0=(−8+j11)3+(2+pc​)(−8+j11)2        +(2+2pc​+K)(−8+j11)+(10K+2pc​)

    0=(2K−71pc​+2262)+j(−154pc​+451+11K)

    #Improving Steady-State Response

    #PI Controller

    Recall that the PI controller is represented as,

    PI=KP​+sKI​​

    PI=KP​(ss+zc​​)

    where 0<zc​<1

    Characteristic equation of transfer function,

    0=1+KP​(ss+0.5​)((s+8)(s+10)2s+5​)

    0=1+KP​[s(s+8)(s+10)2(s+0.5)(s+5)​]

    Zeros and poles,

    z1​=−0.5

    z2​=−5

    p1​=0

    p2​=−8

    p3,4​=−10

    Note that the root locus of the system is not significantly affected by the PI controller.
    The addition of the integral in the open-loop transfer function essentially upgrades
    the system type, and thus eliminates the steady-state error.

    #Lag Compensator

    Lag compensator function is expressed as,

    Lag=K(s+pc​s+zc​​)

    where pc​≪zc​≪1

    Lag compensator increases the position error constant since zc​>pc​.

    KP​=s→0lim​ Gc​(s)⋅G(s)

    Kp​=s→0lim​ K(s+pc​s+zc​​)⋅G(s)

    Kp​=K(pc​zc​​)⋅G(0)

    As the position error constant increases, steady-state error decreases.
