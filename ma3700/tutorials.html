    Mean Cross-Sectional Area

    y1​=2375−250​=62.5 mm

    A1​=21​(250+375)(395.09)

    y2​=2375−125​=125 mm

    x2​=5002−1252​=484.12 mm

    A2​=21​(125+375)(484.12)

    A2​=121031 mm2

    A0​=123465+121031

    A0​=244496 mm2=0.2445 m2

    q=τmax​tmin​

    τmax​=102.25 MN/m2

    Maximum shear stress occurs at the thinnest section.

    #Angle of Twist at Free End

    ϕ=2A0​Gq​∮tds​

    ϕ=4A0​2GT​∮tds​

    where T is a function of z (length of the wing into the paper)

    Torque at the free end is 150 kNm (maximum).

    Let the torque at any section be T=150−25z.

    Sections with Constant Thickness

    ∫AB​tds​+∫DE​tds​=3.5250​+3.5125​

    ∫AB​tds​+∫DE​tds​=107.14

    Sections with Varying Thickness

    Let the expression for BC be t=6−ms.

    At s=400 and t=4.5, m=0.00375.

    tBC​=6−0.00375s

    ∫BC​tBC​ds​

    =∫0400​6−0.00375s1​ ds

    =−0.003751​∫0400​u1​ ds

    =−0.003751​(ln4.5−ln6)

    =76.715

    Let u=6−0.00375s.

    du=−0.00375 ds

    −0.00375du​=ds

    Let the expression for CD be t=4.5−ms.

    At s=500 and t=3, m=0.003.

    tCD​=4.5−0.003s

    ∫CD​tCD​ds​

    =∫0500​4.5−0.003s1​ ds

    =−0.0031​∫0500​u1​ ds

    =−0.0031​(ln3−ln4.5)

    =135.16

    Let u=4.5−0.003s.

    du=−0.003 ds

    −0.003du​=ds

    Angle of Twist

    ∮tds​=107.14+2(76.715)+2(135.16)

    ∮tds​=530.88

    ϕ=4(0.2445)2(28.5×109)530.88(150−25z)​

    ϕ=(77.9×10−9)(150−25z)

    where angle of twist is a function of z

    ϕ=77.9×10−9∫06​150−25z dz

    ϕ=77.9×10−9[150z−12.5z2]06​

    ϕ=0.0000351 rad=0.002°

    Note that each 1m section of the wing twist by a varying amount due to a varying T. Thus, ϕ at the free end is the accumulated twisting of each 1m section, where ϕ(z).

    Moment of inertia about x-axis,

    Ixx​=∑Br​yr​2

    Ixx​=4(500)(200)2

    Ixx​=80×106 mm4

    Moment of inertia about y-axis,

    Iyy​=∑Br​xr​2

    Iyy​=4(500)(50)2

    Iyy​=5×106 mm4

    Equation for shear flow,

    qs​−q0​=−Ixx​Sy​​ ∑Br​yr​−Iyy​Sx​​ ∑Br​xr​

    qs​=−80×106−5000​ ∑Br​yr​−5×1062500​ ∑Br​xr​

    qs​=160001​ ∑Br​yr​−20001​ ∑Br​xr​

    q23​=−6.25+160001​(500)(200)−20001​(500)(−50)

    Practical Aircraft Analysis — Shearing in Idealized Section

    Moment of inertia,

    Ixx​=∑Br​yr​2

    Ixx​=2(645)(102)2+2(1290)(152)2+2(1935)(152)2

    Ixx​=162.44×106 mm4

    Equation for shear flow,

    qs​−q0​=−Ixx​Sy​​ ∑Br​yr​

    Note that q0​=0 for an open section.

    Shear flow in open section

    Shear flow from 1 to 2,

    q12​=−Ixx​Sy​​(645)(102)

    q12​=−Ixx​65790Sy​​

    Since value is negative, shear flow direction is from 2 to 1.

    Shear flow from 2 to 5,

    q25​=q12​−Ixx​Sy​​(1290)(152)

    q25​=−Ixx​65790Sy​​−Ixx​196080Sy​​

    q25​=−Ixx​261870Sy​​

    Since value is negative, shear flow direction is from 5 to 2.

    Shear flow from 5 to 6,

    q56​=q25​−Ixx​Sy​​(1290)(−152)

    q56​=−Ixx​261870Sy​​+Ixx​196080Sy​​

    q56​=−Ixx​65790Sy​​

    Since value is negative, shear flow direction is from 6 to 5.

    Basic shear flow in cell 1

    Cut at the arc between booms 3 and 4, and analyze as an open section.

    Shear flow from 3 to 4,

    q34​=0

    Note that shear flow has not passed through a boom, thus it is zero.

    Shear flow from 4 to 3,

    q43​=−Ixx​Sy​​(1935)(−152)

    q43​=Ixx​294120Sy​​

    Basic shear flow in cell 2

    Cut at the arc between booms 2 and 3, and analyze as an open section.

    Shear flow from 2 to 3,

    q23​=0

    Note that shear flow has not passed through a boom, thus it is zero.

    Shear flow from 3 to 4,

    q34​=−Ixx​Sy​​(1935)(152)

    q34​=−Ixx​294120Sy​​

    Since value is negative, shear flow direction is from 4 to 3.

    Shear flow from 4 to 5,

    q45​=q34​−Ixx​Sy​​(1935)(−152)

    q45​=−Ixx​294120Sy​​+Ixx​294120Sy​​

    q45​=0

    Shear flow from 5 to 2,

    q52​=q45​+q65​−Ixx​Sy​​(1290)(−152)

    q52​=Ixx​65790Sy​​+Ixx​196080Sy​​

    q52​=Ixx​261970Sy​​

    Note that shear flow in wall 56 also contributes to shear flow in wall 52.

    Since vertical load is applied at shear center, angle of twist of the structure is zero. Thus,
    the angle of twist of each cell is also zero, assuming each cell twists with the same angle.

    Equation for rate of twist of multi-cell section,

    ϕ=2AR​G1​[−q0,R−1​δR−1,R​+q0,R​δR​−q0,R+1​δR+1,R​+∮tqb​​ ds]

    Rate of twist for cell 1

    Rate of twist for cell 1,

    0=q0,1​(t34​L34​​+L43​L43​​)−q0,2​(t34​L34​​)+q43​(t43​L43​​)

    0=q0,1​(0.5591015​+2.03304​)−q0,2​(2.03304​)+q43​(2.03304​)

    0=1965.5q0,1​−149.75q0,2​+149.75q43​

    0=13.125q0,1​−q0,2​+q43​

    Rate of twist for cell 2

    Rate of twist for cell 2,

    0=−q0,1​(t43​L43​​)+q0,2​(t23​2L23​​+t43​L43​​+t52​L52​​)+q34​(t43​L43​​)+q52​(t52​L52​​)

    0=−q0,1​(2.03304​)+q0,2​(0.9152(765)​+2.03304​+1.625304​)+q34​(2.03304​)+q52​(1.625304​)

    0=−149.75q0,1​+2008.96q0,2​+149.75q34​+187.08q52​

    0=−0.074541q0,1​+q0,2​+0.074541q34​+0.093123q52​

    0=13.05q0,1​+q43​+0.074541q34​+0.093123q52​

    0=13.05q0,1​+Ixx​294120Sy​​+0.074541(−Ixx​294120Sy​​)+0.093123(Ixx​261970Sy​​)

    0=13.05q0,1​+296591Ixx​Sy​​

    q0,1​=−22726.5Ixx​Sy​​
