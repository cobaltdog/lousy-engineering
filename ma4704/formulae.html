    Forced vibration response,

    ν(x,t)=i=1∑∞​ sin(Liπx​)[Ai​sin(ωi​t)+Bi​cos(ωi​t)+ζi,p​(t)]

    ν(x,t)=i=1∑∞​ ϕi​(x)ζi​(t)

    Θi​(t)=∫0L​ F(x,t)ϕi​(t) dx (Distributed load : integrate as per normal)

    Θi​(t)=Fc​(t)ϕi​(xc​) (Concentrated load : replace x in ϕi​ with location of applied load)

    Θi​(t)=(2ρL​)ζ¨​i​(t)+(2ρL​ωi​2)ζi​(t)

    If generalized force contains sine term, let     ζi,p​(t)=Cp​sin(Ωt).

    ν˙(x,0)=0 or given in Qns

    Substitute initial conditions into vibration response and solve for Ai​ and Bi​.

    #Uniform Beam Torsional Dynamics

    Torsional vibration response,

    θ(y,t)=i=1∑∞​ ϕi​(y)[Ai​sin(ωi​t)+Bi​cos(ωi​t)+ζi,p​(t)]

    where the 3rd term is omitted for free vibration of a beam

    Mode shapes,

    ϕi​(y)=sin(Liπy​) (Clamped-clamped beam)

    ϕi​(y)=sin[2L(2i−1)πy​] (Clamped-free beam)

    βi,FF​=cosh(αi​l)−cos(αi​l)sinh(αi​l)+sin(αi​l)​ (Free-free beam)

    βi,CC​=cosh(αi​l)−cos(αi​l)sinh(αi​l)+sin(αi​l)​ (Clamped-clamped beam)

    #Lagrange Equation of Motion

    Kinetic and potential energy of a beam in bending,

    K=21​∫0L​ ρA(∂t∂ν​)2 dy

    P=21​∫0L​ EI(∂y2∂2ν​)2 dy

    Vibration pattern of a beam,

    ν(y,t)=q(t)ϕ(y)

    Assume vibration pattern to be most representative lowest mode shape.

    Lagrange’s equation,

    L=K−P

    Lagrange equation of motion,

    0=dtd​(∂q˙​i​∂L​)−∂qi​∂L​

    #Pressure Difference

    Local static pressures on upper and lower airfoil surfaces,

    Pupper​=−21​ρ(U+∂x∂ϕu​​)2−ρ∂t∂ϕu​​+Const.

    Plower​=−21​ρ(U+∂x∂ϕl​​)2−ρ∂t∂ϕl​​+Const.

    where velocity potential ϕl​=−ϕu​

    Pressure difference between upper and lower surfaces,

    ΔP=Plower​−Pupper​

    ΔP=2ρ(U∂x∂ϕu​​+∂t∂ϕu​​)
