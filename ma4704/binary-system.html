    #Binary Aeroelastic Wing

    Consider a 3-dimensional wing that is hinged at the root.
    It undergoes bending about the x-axis denoted by κ — positive if it flaps down.
    It undergoes torsion about the elastic axis denoted by θ — positive if it pitches up.

    #Structural Behavior

    Displacement at any point,

    z(x,y,t)=−yκ(t)−(x−xf​)θ(t)

    Assuming downwards displacement is negative.

    Potential energy of supports,

    U=21​Kκ​κ2+21​Kθ​θ2

    Kinetic energy of the wing,

    T=∫0s​∫0c​ 21​mz˙2 dxdy

    T=21​m∫0s​∫0c​ [−yκ˙(t)−(x−xf​)θ˙(t)]2 dxdy

    where mass m is expressed in kg/m2

    Applying Lagrange’s equation,

    L=T−U

    L=21​m∫0s​∫0c​ [−yκ˙(t)−(x−xf​)θ˙(t)]2 dxdy−21​Kκ​κ2−21​Kθ​θ2

    Degree of freedom κ,

    0=dtd​(∂κ˙∂L​)−∂κ∂L​=dtd​{m∫0s​∫0c​ [−yκ˙−(x−xf​)θ˙(t)](−y) dxdy}−Kκ​κ

    0=dtd​(∂κ˙∂L​)−∂κ∂L​=dtd​{m[3s3c​κ˙+2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)θ˙]}−Kκ​κ

    0=dtd​(∂κ˙∂L​)−∂κ∂L​=m[3s3c​κ¨+2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)θ¨]−Kκ​κ

    Degree of freedom θ,

    0=dtd​(∂θ˙∂L​)−∂θ∂L​=dtd​{m∫0s​∫0c​ [−yκ˙−(x−xf​)θ˙(t)](xf​−x) dxdy}−Kθ​θ

    0=dtd​(∂θ˙∂L​)−∂θ∂L​=dtd​{m[2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)κ˙+s(3c3​−c2xf​+xf​2c)θ˙]}−Kθ​θ

    0=dtd​(∂θ˙∂L​)−∂θ∂L​=m[2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)κ¨+s(3c3​−c2xf​+xf​2c)θ¨]−Kθ​θ

    Writing in matrix form, the structural behavior of the wing is obtained.

    💡

    [Iκ​Iκθ​​Iκθ​Iθ​​]{κ¨θ¨​}+[Kκ​0​0Kθ​​]{κθ​}={00​}

    where Iκ​=3ms3c​ and Iκθ​=2ms2​(2c2​−xf​c) and Iθ​=ms(3c3​−c2xf​+xf​2c)

    #Aerodynamic Behavior

    Let e be the distance between the aerodynamic center ac and the elastic axis.

    Sectional lift of the wing,

    dL=21​ρU2c(a1​)(θ+Uκ˙y​)dy

    Total aerodynamic moment,

    dM=21​ρU2c2e(a1​)(θ+Uκ˙y​)dy+21​ρU2c2(Mθ˙​4Uθ˙c​)dy

    1st term represents the moment about the elastic axis due to lift.
    2nd term represents the moment at the aerodynamic center     ac.
    The     Mθ˙​ term represents the unsteady aerodynamics.

    Work done by aerodynamic forces,

    dW=∫0s​ dL(−y dκ)+∫0s​ dM dθ

    where Qκ​≡∂(dκ)∂(dW)​ and Qθ​≡∂(dθ)∂(dW)​

    Generalized forces,

    Qκ​=−21​ρU2cs2(a1​)(2θ​+3Uκ˙s​)

    Qθ​=21​ρU2c2s[ea1​(θ+2Uκ˙s​)+Mθ˙​4Uθ˙c​]

    Writing in matrix form, the aerodynamic behavior of the wing is obtained.

    💡

    {Qκ​Qθ​​}=ρU[−6cs3a1​​4ec2s2a1​​​0Mθ˙​8c3s​​]{κ˙θ˙​}+ρU2[00​−4cs2a1​​2ec2sa1​​​]{κθ​}

    #Aeroelastic Equations of Motion

    Combining the structural and aerodynamic behaviors,

    [Iκ​Iκθ​​Iκθ​Iθ​​]{κ¨θ¨​}+[Kκ​0​0Kθ​​]{κθ​}={Qκ​Qθ​​}

    [A​]{κ¨θ¨​}+[E​]{κθ​}=ρU[B​]{κ˙θ˙​}+ρU2[C​]{κθ​}

    💡

    [A]{q¨​}+[ρU[B]+[D]]{q˙​}+[ρU2[C]+[E]]{q}={0}

    Elements of each matrix

    Mass matrix [A]=[Iκ​Iκθ​​Iκθ​Iθ​​]

    Aerodynamic damping matrix [B]=[6cs3a1​​−4ec2s2a1​​​0−Mθ˙​8c3s​​]

    Aerodynamic stiffness matrix [C]=[00​4cs2a1​​−2ec2sa1​​​]

    Structural damping matrix [D]=[00​00​]

    Structural stiffness matrix [E]=[Kκ​0​0Kθ​​]

    Let {z}={{q}{q˙​}}T and define the relation I{q˙​}−I{q˙​}={0}.

    Aeroelastic equations of motion can be rewritten in 1st order state-space form.

    [I0​0A​]{q˙​q¨​​}−[0−ρU2[C]−[E]​I−ρU[B]−[D]​]{qq˙​​}={00​}

    {q˙​q¨​​}=[I0​0A​]−1[0−ρU2[C]−[E]​I−ρU[B]−[D]​]{qq˙​​}

    {z˙​}=[Q​]{z​}

    Let {z}={Z}eλt and {z˙}=λ{Z}eλt.

    λ{Z}eλt=[Q]{Z}eλt

    [Q−λI]{Z}={0}

    Eigenvalue problem.

    Natural frequencies,

    💡

    ωi​=∣λi​∣=Re2+Im2​

    Damping ratios,

    💡

    ζi​=ωi​−Re(λi​)​

    #Flutter Speed Prediction Method

    Let {q}={Q}eλt.

    Aeroelastic equations of motion,

    [A]{q¨​}+[ρU[B]+[D]]{q˙​}+[ρU2[C]+[E]]{q}={0}

    [λ2[A]+λ[ρU[B]+[D]]+[ρU2[C]+[E]]]{Q}={0}

    [a11​λ2+b11​Uλ+c11​U2+e11​a21​λ2+b21​Uλ+c21​U2​a12​λ2+b12​Uλ+c12​U2a22​λ2+b22​Uλ+c22​U2+e12​​]{Q1​Q2​​}={00​}

    Equate determinant of the above matrix to zero to obtain the characteristic equation.

    💡

    0=s4​λ4+s3​λ3+s2​λ2+s1​λ+s0​

    Solution to the characteristic equation is in the form of complex conjugate pairs.

    💡

    λ=−ζω±iω1−ζ2​

    Coefficients of characteristic equation,

    s4​=a11​a22​−a21​a12​

    s3​=(a11​b22​+b11​a22​−a21​b12​−a12​b21​)U

    s2​=[(μa11​+a22​)x+a11​c22​+b11​b22​+c11​a22​−a21​c12​−b12​b21​−c21​a12​]U2

    s1​=[(μb11​+b22​)x+b11​c22​+c11​b22​−b21​c12​−c21​b12​]U3

    s0​=[μx2+(c22​+μc11​)x+c11​c22​−c12​c21​]U4

    where μ=e11​e22​​ and e11​=xU2 and e22​=μxU2

    Flutter occurs when damping becomes zero such that λ=±iω.

    Substituting λ=iω into the characteristic equation,

    0=s4​(iω)4+s3​(iω)3+s2​(iω)2+s1​(iω)+s0​

    0=s4​ω4−is3​ω3−s2​ω2+is1​ω+s0​

    Substituting λ=−iω into the characteristic equation,

    0=s4​(−iω)4+s3​(−iω)3+s2​(−iω)2+s1​(−iω)+s0​

    0=s4​ω4+is3​ω3−s2​ω2−is1​ω+s0​

    Subtraction of the above equations,

    0=is3​ω3−is1​ω

    0=s3​ω2−s1​

    Flutter frequencies,

    💡

    ωF1,2​=s3​s1​​​

    Addition of the above equations,

    0=s4​ω4−s2​ω2+s0​

    0=s4​ωF​4−s2​ωF​2+s0​

    Flutter speeds,

    💡

    UF1,2​=x1,2​e11​​​

    #Control Surface Flutter

    Consider a 3-dimensional wing with a full-span control surface.
    The flap undergoes deflection denoted by β — positive for downwards deflection.
    The wing undergoes bending about the x-axis denoted by κ — positive if it flaps down.
    The wing undergoes torsion about the elastic axis denoted by θ — positive if it pitches up.

    #Structural Behavior

    Displacement at any point,

    z(x,y,t)=−yκ(t)−(x−xf​)θ(t)−[x−xβ​]β(t)

    where [x−xβ​]=x if x>xβ​ and [x−xβ​]=0 if x<xβ​

    Potential energy of supports and flap,

    U=21​Kκ​κ2+21​Kθ​θ2+21​Kβ​β2

    Kinetic energy of the wing and flap,

    T=∫0s​∫0c​ 21​mz˙2 dxdy

    T=21​m∫0s​∫0c​ [−yκ˙(t)−(x−xf​)θ˙(t)−[x−xβ​]β˙​(t)]2dxdy

    Applying Lagrange’s equation,

    L=T−U

    Degree of freedom κ,

    0=dtd​(∂κ˙∂L​)−∂κ∂L​

    0=m[3s3c​κ¨+2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)θ¨+2s2​(2c2​−xβ​c+2xβ​2​)β¨​]−Kκ​κ

    Degree of freedom θ,

    0=dtd​(∂θ˙∂L​)−∂θ∂L​

    0=m[2s2​(2c2​−xf​c)κ¨             +s(3c3​−xf​c2+xf​2c)θ¨             +s(3c3​+6xβ​3​−2xf​c2​−2xf​xβ​2​+xf​xβ​c−2c2xβ​​)β¨​]−Kθ​θ

    Degree of freedom β,

    0=dtd​(∂β˙​∂L​)−∂β∂L​

    0=m[2s2​(2c2​−xβ​c+2xβ​2​)κ¨             +s(3c3​+6xβ​3​−2xf​c2​−2xf​xβ​2​+xf​xβ​c−2c2xβ​​)θ¨             +s(3c3​−3xβ​3​−c2xβ​+cxβ​2)β¨​]−Kβ​β

    Writing in matrix form, the structural behavior of the wing is obtained.

    💡

    ​Iκ​Iκθ​Iκβ​​Iκθ​Iθ​Iθβ​​Iκβ​Iθβ​Iβ​​​⎩⎨⎧​κ¨θ¨β¨​​⎭⎬⎫​+​Kκ​00​0Kθ​0​00Kβ​​​⎩⎨⎧​κθβ​⎭⎬⎫​=⎩⎨⎧​000​⎭⎬⎫​

    #Aerodynamic Behavior

    Let E=cc−xβ​​ and d=c−12xβ​​.

    Let T10​=1−d2​+cos−1(d).

    Let T12​=(2+d)1−d2​+(2+d)cos−1(d).

    Sectional lift of the wing,

    dL=21​ρU2c[aw​(θ+Uκ˙y​)+ac​β]dy

    where aw​=2π and ac​=2[cos−1(1−2E)+2E(1−E)​]

    Aerodynamic moment about elastic axis,

    dMfa​=21​ρU2c2[bw​(θ+Uκ˙y​)+bc​β+Mθ˙​4Uθ˙c​]dy

    where bw​=eaw​ and bc​=−2(1−E)E(1−E)​

    Aerodynamic moment about flap hinge axis,

    dMha​=21​ρU2c2[cw​(θ+Uκ˙y​)+cc​β+Mβ˙​​4Uβ˙​c​]y

    where cw​=−2T12​​ and cc​=2πT12​T10​​

    Work done by aerodynamic forces,

    dW=∫0s​ dL(−y dκ)+∫0s​ dMfa​ dθ+∫0s​ dMha​ dβ

    where Qκ​≡∂(dκ)∂(dW)​ and Qθ​≡∂(dθ)∂(dW)​ and Qβ​≡∂(dβ)∂(dW)​

    Writing in matrix form, the aerodynamic behavior of the wing is obtained.

    💡

    b11​=6cs3aw​​b21​=−4cs2bw​​, b22​=−8c2Mθ˙​​b31​=−4cs2cw​​, b33​=−8c2Mβ˙​​​
